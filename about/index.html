About Mobile Ustaad

An independent, information-only guide to mobile phones in Pakistan.

What we do

Mobile Ustaad helps you choose the right phone at the right price in Pakistan. We publish:

  • Full phone specifications — checked field by field against trusted sources.
  • Pakistan prices — a verified snapshot of the market; every price carries its source and updated date.
  • Problem-solving guides — practical fixes for common phone issues.
  • Upcoming phones — launches to watch, with expected prices where available.
  • Spare parts — typical market prices and repair guidance.

How we verify prices

Prices are cross-checked across PriceOye, WhatMobile, HamariWeb, Daraz, official brand stores and market reports. We update them daily and label each price with its source and date — because a stale price breaks trust.

What we don't do

We are an information site only: no downloads, no APK links, no piracy. We don't sell phones — we help you decide before you buy.

Who runs Mobile Ustaad

Mobile Ustaad is owned and run by Shoaib Hashmi, who researches, verifies and publishes the prices and specifications on this site himself. Every listing is checked against official brand sources and established Pakistani market sources before it goes live, and prices are re-checked daily — if a figure cannot be verified, we say so on the page instead of guessing.

Spotted a wrong price or spec? Want a phone added? Use our contact page — we read every message.

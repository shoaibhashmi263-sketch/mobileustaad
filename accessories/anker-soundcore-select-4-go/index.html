Our Verdict

The Soundcore Select 4 Go is a pocket speaker built for water, not for parties. Its best trick is genuine: an IP67 waterproof and dustproof body that actually floats, so it can survive poolside afternoons, beach trips and bathroom shelves without drama. Battery life is another strong point at up to 20 hours of playtime, and two units can pair in TWS for true stereo sound. The trade-off is power — 5W is enough for a desk, a picnic or a small room, not for a gathering. At Rs 6,349 it is a fair price for the waterproofing, battery life and Anker build quality. Buy it for travel and outdoor use. If you mostly listen at home and want bass and volume, put the money toward a larger speaker instead. For buyers here, the appeal is practical: it is cheap enough to toss into a gym bag or take on a picnic without worrying about rain, sand or a fall into water — conditions that finish off ordinary speakers.

FAQs

What is the Anker Soundcore Select 4 Go price in Pakistan?

The Anker Soundcore Select 4 Go is priced at Rs 6,349 in Pakistan, based on PriceOye listings tracked on this page (price updated October 2026).

Is the Soundcore Select 4 Go really waterproof?

Yes. The Select 4 Go is rated IP67 for water and dust resistance, which covers splashes, rain and brief immersion. Unusually for a speaker, its design also floats, so a drop into the pool does not mean losing it to the bottom.

How long does the Select 4 Go battery last?

Soundcore lists up to 20 hours of playtime for the Select 4 Go. Real-world life depends on volume — loud listening drains it faster — but even so, it is one of the longer-lasting speakers in this size class. A full charge comfortably covers a weekend trip of daily listening.

Can I pair two Select 4 Go speakers together?

Yes. With a second Select 4 Go, TWS pairing links the two units as left and right channels for proper stereo sound. A single unit on its own plays in mono.

Is a 5W speaker loud enough?

For personal listening, yes. The 5W driver is designed for a desk, a small room, a picnic blanket or a bathroom shelf, and it stays clear at close range. It will not fill a large hall or an outdoor gathering — for that, look at a bigger speaker.

What comes with the Soundcore Select 4 Go?

A strap is included, which suits its travel-first design — clip it to a bag, a bicycle or a shower hook and take it along. The speaker itself is small enough to carry in a jacket pocket.

Does the Select 4 Go have a microphone for phone calls?

Its listed specs do not mention a built-in microphone or speakerphone function, so treat it as a music-only speaker and take calls on the phone itself. For most buyers at this price, that is a fair trade for the waterproofing.

Soundcore Select 4 Go or a bigger party speaker — which should I buy?

Buy the Select 4 Go if your listening happens outdoors, while travelling, or anywhere near water — that is what it is designed for. Buy a bigger speaker if it will mostly live at home and you care about bass, volume and room-filling sound.

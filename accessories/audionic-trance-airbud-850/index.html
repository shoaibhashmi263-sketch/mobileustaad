Our Verdict

The Audionic Trance Airbud 850 is the step-up model in the Trance line: Hexa Mic ANC, 13mm drivers, up to 50 hours of total playtime and a 45ms gaming mode, with custom EQ through the Audionic Suite app. In Pakistan it sells for Rs 7,999 (source: audionic.co, the official store, price updated 2026-11-08). For roughly Rs 2,500 more than the Airbud 820 you get stronger call microphones, app support and IPX5 resistance — a sensible pick if calls and gaming matter to you.

FAQs

What is the Audionic Trance Airbud 850 price in Pakistan?

The Audionic Trance Airbud 850 price in Pakistan is Rs 7,999 (source: audionic.co, updated 2026-11-08). Prices vary a little between stores and cities, so confirm the final price and warranty before ordering.

What is the difference between the Airbud 850 and the Airbud 820?

The 850 steps up to six ANC microphones (the 820 has two), larger 13mm drivers (vs 10mm), app-based custom EQ and IPX5 resistance (vs IPX4), and a faster 45ms gaming mode — but its total playtime is 50 hours against the 820's 60.

How long does the Airbud 850 battery last?

Audionic lists up to 50 hours of total playtime with the case. The buds carry 40mAh cells and the case 500mAh, charged over USB-C.

How can I check if the Airbud 850 is original?

Buy from audionic.co or an authorized dealer, and check that the packaging, serial number and warranty card are present — it carries a 1-year warranty. Prices far below the going rate are usually copies or grey imports.

A standing warning for this market: accessories are the most copied products in Pakistan. A price far below the going rate on any page here almost always means a copy or a grey import without warranty — every accessory page explains what to check, and our phone pages link the accessories that pair with each model. For chargers and cables, match the connector and wattage to your phone; the phone, not the charger, sets the charging speed.

Our Verdict

The JBL Tune Beam 2 is built for bass lovers who want JBL's signature sound without paying flagship money. At Rs 26,500 in Pakistan you get active noise cancellation, a huge 48-hour total battery reserve and the JBL Headphones app with a proper EQ — a combination few rivals match at this price. Call quality is decent and the beam-style fit stays put during commutes. Buy it if punchy, fun sound and long battery matter most to you. Skip it if you need the absolute best call clarity in noisy streets or wireless charging — and compare it with the cheaper Wave Buds 2 first, which shares much of the DNA.

Pros & Cons

FAQs

What is the JBL Tune Beam 2 price in Pakistan?

The JBL Tune Beam 2 is priced at Rs 26,500 in Pakistan (price updated 2026-10-01, via myshop.pk). Prices can vary slightly between sellers, so check a couple of authorized retailers before buying.

Does the JBL Tune Beam 2 have official warranty in Pakistan?

What is the difference between the JBL Tune Beam 2 and Wave Buds 2?

The Tune Beam 2 offers 48-hour total battery and a beam (stick) design at Rs 26,500, while the Wave Buds 2 has larger 8mm drivers, IP54 rating and 4 microphones at a lower Rs 16,990. Pick the Beam 2 for battery life, the Wave Buds 2 for bigger sound per rupee.

How is the battery life in real use?

JBL rates the Tune Beam 2 at 48 hours total including the charging case. With ANC on at moderate volume, expect roughly 7–8 hours per bud charge, with the case topping you up several times over.

Is the JBL Tune Beam 2 good for gaming and calls?

Casual gaming is fine, though like most Bluetooth earbuds there is slight audio latency — competitive gamers should use a wired option. Calls are clear indoors thanks to the mic array; very noisy outdoor environments are its weak spot.

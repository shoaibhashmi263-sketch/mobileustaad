Our Verdict

The JBL Wave Buds 2 is one of the easiest earbud recommendations under Rs 17,000 in Pakistan. You get bigger 8mm drivers than its pricier sibling, real ANC, IP54 dust-and-water resistance and four microphones for calls — a spec sheet that embarrasses many costlier rivals. Ten hours per charge means you will rarely reach for the case during a full day out. Buy it if you want maximum sound and features per rupee. Skip it only if you specifically need wireless charging or the absolute longest total battery, where the Tune Beam 2 pulls ahead.

FAQs

What is the JBL Wave Buds 2 price in Pakistan?

The JBL Wave Buds 2 costs Rs 16,990 in Pakistan (price updated 2026-10-01, via Vmart.pk). It is one of the best-value ANC earbuds in this bracket.

Is the JBL Wave Buds 2 waterproof?

It carries an IP54 rating, meaning it resists dust and water splashes — safe for workouts and light rain, but not for swimming or submersion.

Which is better: JBL Wave Buds 2 or Tune Beam 2?

The Wave Buds 2 wins on driver size (8mm vs 6mm), call mics (4 vs fewer) and price (Rs 16,990 vs Rs 26,500). The Tune Beam 2 wins on total battery (48h vs 40h) and offers a different beam-style fit. Most buyers get more for their money with the Wave Buds 2.

How many microphones does the Wave Buds 2 have?

Four microphones with noise-reduction algorithms, which is why call quality is a genuine strength at this price.

Does it support fast charging?

Yes — a quick top-up in the case gives hours of playback, handy when you are heading out and the buds are low.

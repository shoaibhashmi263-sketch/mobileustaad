FAQs

What is the Samsung Galaxy Buds 4 Pro price in Pakistan?

The Samsung Galaxy Buds 4 Pro price in Pakistan is Rs 49,999 (source: Shophive, model SM-R640, updated 2026-11-05). Prices vary between stores, so confirm the final price and warranty before ordering.

How long does the Galaxy Buds 4 Pro battery last?

Samsung rates the buds at 6 hours with ANC on, and 26 hours in total including the charging case. The case supports wireless charging as well as USB-C.

Do the Galaxy Buds 4 Pro work with non-Samsung phones?

Yes — they pair over Bluetooth 5.4 with any phone for music and calls. The 24-bit/96kHz Samsung Seamless Codec, however, works with Samsung Galaxy devices; other phones use standard codecs.

Are the Galaxy Buds 4 Pro waterproof?

The buds are rated IP57, which covers dust and immersion in fresh water up to 1 metre for 30 minutes. The rating applies to the buds, not the charging case, and it is meant for accidents and workouts rather than swimming.

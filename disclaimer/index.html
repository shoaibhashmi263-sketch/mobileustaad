Mobile phone prices in Pakistan change often — sometimes within days. Prices also vary between shops and cities, and between variants of the same phone (RAM, storage, PTA-approved or not).

2. We do not sell phones

Mobile Ustaad is an information website only. We do not sell phones or accessories, we hold no stock, and we cannot book, reserve or deliver a product for you. Any purchase is made directly between you and the seller, on the seller's terms.

3. Where our information comes from

Specifications and prices are compiled from third-party sources — official brand pages and stores, and established Pakistani market sources such as PriceOye, WhatMobile and GSMArena-style specification databases. We cross-check the figures we publish, but we cannot guarantee that every specification is complete or free of error, and manufacturers sometimes change specifications between regions or production runs without notice.

4. Expected and rumored prices

For phones that have not officially launched in Pakistan, we may show an expected price based on announced prices in other markets or reputable pre-launch reporting. Such prices are always labeled "Expected", and the confirmed Pakistan price at launch may be higher or lower.

5. Confirm before you buy

Before paying for any phone, please confirm with the seller: the final price, the exact variant, whether the unit is PTA-approved (and whether PTA tax is included), and the warranty terms. If you find a price or specification on this site that looks wrong, tell us through our contact page and we will re-check it.

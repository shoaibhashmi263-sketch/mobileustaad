Apps keep crashing

An app opens and immediately closes, or you see a 'keeps stopping' message — try these fixes.

Why apps crash

An app usually crashes for one of a few simple reasons: the app itself has a bug, its temporary files (cache) have become corrupted, the phone is running low on storage or memory, or the app is no longer fully compatible with your phone's software version. Sometimes a weak or unstable internet connection also makes apps close, especially banking, shopping and social apps that need to talk to a server the moment they open. The good news is that most crashes are software problems you can fix at home in a few minutes — follow the steps below in order, from the quickest to the most thorough.

Steps

  1. Update the app
    Play Store > profile > Manage apps — install the update for the crashing app. Developers often ship crash fixes in updates.
  2. Clear the cache
    Settings > Apps > [app name] > Storage > Clear Cache. Your app data stays safe; only temporary files are cleared.
  3. Clear app data (last easy resort)
    If clearing the cache didn't help, use 'Clear Data' — but remember, it makes the app like a fresh install (you'll have to log in again).
  4. Check permissions
    Settings > Apps > [app] > Permissions — many apps crash when required permissions (Storage, Camera) are denied.
  5. Reinstall it
    Uninstall the app and install it again from the Play Store. A corrupt install sometimes gets fixed this way.
  6. Phone software update
    Settings > System > Software Update — once system-level bugs are fixed, apps become stable too.
  7. Restart the phone
    A restart clears stuck background processes. If several apps started crashing at the same time, restart before trying anything more drastic.
  8. Free up storage space
    If your storage is almost full, apps cannot save the temporary files they need and start crashing. Delete old videos and unused apps, and keep at least 1–2 GB free.
  9. Update Android System WebView
    Many apps show web content through a system component called Android System WebView. If lots of unrelated apps crash together, update both 'Android System WebView' and Chrome from the Play Store — a faulty WebView version is a classic cause of mass crashes.

If only one app crashes

If every other app works fine and just one app keeps closing, the problem is almost certainly inside that app — a bad update, corrupt data or a bug linked to your account. Clear its cache and data as described above, then reinstall it. If it still crashes after a fresh install, open the app's Play Store page and read the recent reviews: if other users are reporting the same crash, only the developer can fix it. The practical solution is to wait for the next update, or use the service's website version in your browser for a few days.

If many apps crash at the same time

When several unrelated apps all start crashing on the same day, look for a system-level cause instead of fixing apps one by one. Update Android System WebView and Chrome, install any pending phone software update, and restart the phone. Also check your storage: a completely full phone makes many apps fail together. If the crashes began right after you installed something new, uninstall that app — a badly behaved app can disturb others running in the background, especially VPN, antivirus and 'cleaner' apps.

When to visit a service centre

If apps keep crashing even after a factory reset, or the phone also restarts on its own, gets unusually hot, or the battery drains much faster than before, the problem may be hardware — such as a failing storage chip or battery. That is the point to visit an authorised service centre instead of trying more software fixes. One more caution: never install banking or payment apps from outside the Play Store, and if a banking app behaves strangely, contact your bank before using it again.

How to prevent crashes

  • Turn on auto-updates in the Play Store so crash fixes install themselves.
  • Keep at least 10–15% of your storage free at all times.
  • Install apps only from the Play Store — modified APK files from random websites crash often and can be unsafe.
  • Restart your phone once a week to clear stuck processes before they cause trouble.

Tips

  • Beta versions of apps are unstable — opt out of the beta program in the Play Store.
  • Running 2 VPN apps at the same time can cause crashes.

Oukitel WP63 — 20,000mAh, and every compromise that implies

The Oukitel WP63 (Rs 93,999, market price) carries a battery four times the size of a normal phone's. It is a rugged phone built for people who work outdoors, travel off-grid, or simply refuse to carry a power bank: the catalog lists a 64MP main camera plus an 8MP night-vision camera with infrared lights, a 6.67-inch 120Hz display and 33W charging — plus 18W reverse wired charging, so the phone itself can charge other devices.

Understand the trade before buying. Rugged phones are bulky and heavy by design, the Unisoc T8200 is a mid-range processor, and this is a market (import) listing rather than an official Pakistan launch — check PTA approval status carefully before paying, using our PTA approval guide. For the right buyer, nothing else on this page comes close; for everyone else, it is far more phone — literally — than they need.

  • Walk past it if: you want a phone that disappears into a pocket.
  • Look instead at: the Vivo Y500.

Vivo Y500 — the biggest battery in a normal phone

The Vivo Y500 (Rs 99,999, official vivo Pakistan price) fits 8,100mAh into a conventional smartphone with a 6.83-inch AMOLED display at 120Hz — the best screen of any phone on this page — and charges it at 44W. The Dimensity 7300e processor, 6GB of RAM and 128GB of storage are honest mid-range hardware, and the 50MP main camera uses a Sony sensor.

This is the pick for buyers who want maximum endurance without a rugged phone's bulk or an import's uncertainty: it is officially sold in Pakistan with proper warranty. The price is the only real objection.

  • Walk past it if: Rs 100,000 is beyond reach — the same endurance exists cheaper below.
  • Look instead at: the Realme C100x.

Tecno Pova Curve 2 5G — 8,000mAh without the brick

Tecno's answer to the battery problem is engineering rather than size: the Pova Curve 2 5G (Rs 99,999, official Tecno Pakistan launch price) packs 8,000mAh behind a curved 6.78-inch AMOLED display running at 144Hz, with a Dimensity 7100 processor, 8GB of RAM, 256GB of storage and 45W charging. It is a gaming-leaning phone that also happens to outlast almost everything.

Its rear camera — a 50MP main plus a 2MP macro — is ordinary for the price, so photographers should look at the Y500. Everyone else gets 5G, a flagship-style screen and two-day endurance in one official, warrantied package.

  • Walk past it if: camera quality outranks screen and endurance for you.
  • Look instead at: the Vivo Y500.

Lenovo Legion Y70 — flagship speed, 90W charging, half the price

The Legion Y70 (Rs 43,500, market price) is the value outlier of this guide: a Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 gaming phone with a 6.82-inch 2K LTPO OLED display at 144Hz, 12GB of RAM, 256GB of storage — and an 8,000mAh battery that charges at 90W, the fastest top-up on this page. A flat battery stops being a crisis when a short charge restores hours of use.

One honest catch: this is an imported gaming phone sold at market prices, not an official Pakistan launch. Verify PTA approval and warranty terms with the seller before buying — the PTA guide shows how.
  • Walk past it if: you only buy official, locally warrantied phones.
  • Look instead at: the Tecno Pova Curve 2 5G.

Realme C100x — 8,000mAh on a budget

The Realme C100x (Rs 67,299, PriceOye listing) brings the 8,000mAh class down in price, with 45W charging, a 6.8-inch 120Hz display, 6GB of RAM and 128GB of storage. The savings show in two places: the display is HD+ LCD rather than AMOLED, and the Unisoc T7250 is an entry-level processor under a big phone's ambitions.

For delivery riders, field workers and anyone whose day is longer than a normal battery, those are acceptable trades. For gamers, they are not.

  • Walk past it if: you play demanding games.
  • Look instead at: the Legion Y70 if you accept an import, or save more for the Pova Curve 2 5G.

Vivo Y28 — the cheapest way onto this page

At Rs 38,999 (PriceOye listing), the Vivo Y28 is the affordable pick: 6,000mAh is still a third more than a standard battery, and 44W charging — faster than phones costing twice as much — means it refills quickly too. You also get 8GB of RAM, 128GB of storage and a 50MP main camera.

The screen is a 6.68-inch HD+ LCD at 90Hz and the Helio G85 is an older, modest processor. But as a pure endurance-per-rupee calculation, nothing else here beats it.

  • Walk past it if: display sharpness matters to you — every pick above it has a better screen.
  • Step up to: the Realme C100x for the full 8,000mAh.

How to read battery claims before you buy

Three rules keep you honest in the shop. First, capacity is not endurance: an efficient processor on an 8,000mAh battery (the Y500, the Pova Curve 2) will outlast a thirsty one. Second, charging watts decide how painful a flat battery is — 44W and above turns an emergency top-up into minutes, not an hour. Third, if your phone charges slowly despite a fast rating, the fault is often the cable or adapter, not the phone — our slow charging guide walks through the checks. Pick the battery size your day actually needs, and spend what is left on the screen and processor you will notice every hour.

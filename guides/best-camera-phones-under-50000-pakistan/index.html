Best Camera Phones Under Rs 50,000 in Pakistan (2026)

Camera shopping under Rs 50,000 in Pakistan runs on one misleading number: megapixels. A 108MP badge helps — it buys sharper daylight photos and room to crop — but stabilisation, sensor quality and whether you get an ultrawide matter just as much. This list mixes both kinds of pick: the 108MP phones, and one 50MP phone with optical image stabilisation that will beat them after dark.

Every price is from our catalog, checked 18 November 2026. And if your current phone's photos have gone soft or blurry, the fault may be fixable — start with our blurry photos guide before buying anything.

Redmi 13 4G — 108MP for Rs 35,999

The Redmi 13 4G (Rs 35,999, official Xiaomi store price) is the cheapest route to a 108MP main camera in Pakistan. Around it you get a 6.79-inch 90Hz display, a Helio G91 Ultra processor, 8GB of RAM, 128GB of storage, a 5,030mAh battery and 33W charging — a complete phone, not a camera with a phone attached.

There is no ultrawide and no stabilisation beyond software, so night shots need a steady hand. In daylight, where most photos happen, the big sensor earns its place.

  • Walk past it if: you shoot mostly at night or indoors.
  • Look instead at: the Realme 12 4G and its OIS.

Itel S24 — the camera plus the storage to keep everything

The Itel S24 (Rs 36,999, PriceOye listing) pairs its 108MP main camera with something photographers actually run out of: 256GB of storage, double what rivals offer at this price, alongside 8GB of RAM. The 6.6-inch display runs at 90Hz and the battery is 5,000mAh with 18W charging.

The Helio G91 is a budget processor and the screen is a plain IPS LCD. But as a photo-first package under Rs 37,000 — shoot a lot, keep everything, never delete — it is well aimed.

  • Walk past it if: display quality matters as much as the camera.
  • Look instead at: the Redmi Note 13 4G.

Realme 12 4G — fewer megapixels, better photographs

The Realme 12 4G (Rs 42,499, PriceOye listing) is the connoisseur's pick here. Its 50MP main camera uses a Sony LYT-600 sensor with optical image stabilisation — the only OIS in this guide — which means sharper low-light shots and steadier video than any 108MP rival on this page can manage without it. The rest is strong too: a 6.67-inch AMOLED display at 120Hz, a Snapdragon 685, 8GB of RAM, 256GB of storage and the fastest charging here, 67W.

There is no ultrawide camera, and daylight detail will trail the 108MP phones when you crop in. For real-world photos — evenings, indoors, moving children — this is the best camera under Rs 50,000 in our catalog.

  • Walk past it if: you mostly shoot landscapes and want an ultrawide.
  • Look instead at: the Redmi Note 13 4G.

Redmi Note 13 4G — three cameras and the best screen here

The Redmi Note 13 4G (Rs 42,999, official Xiaomi store price) is the most versatile camera system on this page: a 108MP main camera, an 8MP ultrawide for group shots and buildings, a 2MP macro, and a 16MP selfie camera. Photos are viewed best here too, on a 6.67-inch AMOLED display at 120Hz — a panel nothing else at this price matches.

It is a 2024 model, so its two listed OS updates mean a shorter remaining software runway than newer rivals, and there is no OIS. As a stills package with a screen to enjoy them on, it remains the one to beat.

  • Walk past it if: low-light and video matter most.
  • Look instead at: the Realme 12 4G.

Honor X7c — 108MP that lasts all weekend

The Honor X7c (Rs 44,999, Pakistan retail listings) combines the 108MP main camera with the biggest battery in this guide — 5,200mAh with 35W charging — plus a 6.77-inch 120Hz display, a Snapdragon 685, 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage. For travel and event days, when the camera works hardest and chargers are far away, that pairing is the argument.

Like the other 108MP phones here it has no OIS and no ultrawide. It is a daylight-and-endurance camera phone.

  • Walk past it if: you want an ultrawide or stabilised video.
  • Look instead at: the Redmi Note 13 4G for the ultrawide, the Realme 12 4G for OIS.

Itel S25 Ultra — the selfie specialist

Most budget phones treat the front camera as an afterthought; the Itel S25 Ultra (Rs 44,999, PriceOye listing) leads with a 32MP selfie camera — double or more what rivals here offer — in front of a 6.78-inch AMOLED display at 120Hz with an in-display fingerprint reader. The 50MP rear camera is competent, and 8GB of RAM with 256GB of storage rounds out a strong package for creators on a budget.

Charging is 18W and the Unisoc T620 is a modest processor. If your camera faces you more often than the world, this is your phone in this guide.

  • Walk past it if: rear-camera versatility is the priority.
  • Look instead at: the Redmi Note 13 4G.

What no camera phone under Rs 50,000 does well

Set expectations before you buy: none of these phones has a telephoto lens, none shoots flagship-grade video, and low-light results trail the daylight ones everywhere except, partly, the OIS-equipped Realme 12. Within those limits, pick by how you actually shoot — detail and cropping (108MP picks), evenings and video (Realme 12), group shots (Note 13's ultrawide), or selfies (S25 Ultra).

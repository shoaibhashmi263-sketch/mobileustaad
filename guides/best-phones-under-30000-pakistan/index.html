Best Phones Under Rs 30,000 in Pakistan (2026)

Under Rs 30,000 is where most Pakistanis actually buy their phones, and it is also the price band where the spec sheets lie the hardest. Almost everything here advertises a big screen and a big battery; the real differences are the processor, the charging speed, and whether the phone supports 5G. Two phones on this page do — something that was unheard of at this price a year ago.

Every price below is taken from our catalog and checked on 18 November 2026. Where a price is a market price rather than an official one, we say so — and as always, buy a PTA-approved unit from a proper seller; our PTA approval guide explains what to check before you pay.

Infinix Hot 60i 5G — the most complete phone under Rs 30,000

The Hot 60i 5G (Rs 29,999, market price checked 18 November 2026) is the only phone on this page that ticks every box at once: a MediaTek Dimensity 6400 processor with real 5G support, 4GB of RAM and a generous 128GB of storage, a 6,000mAh battery, a 50MP triple rear camera and a 6.75-inch IPS screen running at 120Hz. On paper, nothing else under Rs 30,000 in Pakistan combines all of that.

The compromises are where Infinix saved the money. Charging is 18W, which is slow for a 6,000mAh battery — expect long top-ups — and the selfie camera is only 5MP. The processor is an efficient 6nm chip rather than a gaming chip, so it suits everyday use and light games, not heavy titles.

  • Walk past it if: you charge in short bursts and need fast top-ups more than a huge battery.
  • Look instead at: the Honor X6c, which charges almost twice as fast.

Honor X6c — for buyers who hate waiting at the socket

The Honor X6c (Rs 29,999, market price) makes its case with two numbers: 35W charging — the fastest on this page — and 6GB of RAM, the most on this page, paired with 128GB of storage. Its 5,300mAh battery is a little smaller than the Infinix's, but it refills far quicker, which many people will feel more in daily life.

The screen is a 6.61-inch TFT LCD at 120Hz — smooth, but TFT panels do not match IPS for viewing angles and contrast. The rear camera is a 50MP unit, the front camera 5MP, and there is no 5G: the Helio G81 Ultra is a 4G processor. Buy it for speed of charging and memory, not for future-proofing.

  • Walk past it if: 5G support matters to you, or you watch a lot of video and want a better panel.
  • Look instead at: the Infinix Hot 60i 5G.

Tecno Spark Go 3 — the newest model on the page

The Spark Go 3 (Rs 29,999, Tecno Pakistan price) is a 2026 model, which counts for something at this level: it ships with Android 15, a 6.75-inch IPS display at 120Hz, 4GB of RAM and 128GB of storage. Tecno's official presence in Pakistan also means straightforward warranty and service, which is worth real money on a budget phone.

Be clear about what it is not. The Unisoc T7250 is an entry-level processor, the rear camera is a basic 13MP unit, and 15W charging for the 5,000mAh battery is unhurried. This is a phone for calls, WhatsApp, YouTube and social media — bought with eyes open, it does that job well.

  • Walk past it if: camera quality matters to you at all.
  • Look instead at: the Itel City 200, which costs less and carries a 50MP camera.

Itel City 200 — a 50MP camera for Rs 27,999

Itel built its name in Pakistan on exactly this formula: one headline feature rivals charge more for, in a phone that undercuts them. The City 200 (Rs 27,999, itel Pakistan price) puts a 50MP main camera and a 6.78-inch 120Hz display into a phone that costs less than everything above it here, with a 5,200mAh battery, 18W charging, 4GB of RAM, 128GB of storage and Android 15.

The Unisoc T7250 processor is the same entry-level chip as the Tecno's, so performance is everyday-grade. But if your priorities are photos, screen size and battery at the lowest possible price, this is the sharpest value on the page.

  • Walk past it if: you play games beyond the casual level.
  • Look instead at: the Infinix Hot 60i 5G and its Dimensity 6400.

Oppo A3x — 5G and 45W charging, with one clear catch

The Oppo A3x (Rs 27,999, market price) is the second 5G phone here, and it pairs its Dimensity 6300 processor with the fastest charging in this guide — 45W into a 5,100mAh battery — plus a 6.67-inch 120Hz display and 128GB of storage. For connectivity and charging, it is arguably the strongest package under Rs 30,000.

The catch, plainly: the main rear camera is only 8MP — by far the weakest camera on this page. If you take photos regularly, this is the wrong phone however good the rest looks.

Buy it if your phone is a hotspot, a browser and a messaging machine first, and a camera a distant second.

  • Walk past it if: you photograph family events, documents or products to sell online.
  • Look instead at: the Itel City 200 at the same money.

Samsung Galaxy A06 — the floor for a phone from a global brand

At Rs 22,499 (PriceOye listing, checked 18 November 2026), the Galaxy A06 is the cheapest phone on this page by a wide margin, and the only Samsung. You get a 50MP rear camera, a 5,000mAh battery with 25W charging, a Helio G85 processor with 4GB of RAM and 64GB of storage — and, unusually for this price, a published update policy of two OS updates and four years of security updates in our catalog.

The screen is a basic HD+ PLS LCD, and 64GB fills up fast if you shoot video. But for a first smartphone, a phone for an elder, or a dependable second phone, the combination of brand support and price is hard to argue with.

  • Walk past it if: you can stretch to Rs 28–30,000 — every phone above it here is meaningfully better.
  • Step up to: the Itel City 200 or the Hot 60i 5G.

What Rs 30,000 still cannot buy

No phone here has an AMOLED screen, optical image stabilisation, or a genuinely fast processor — and only two have 5G. Software support is short or unpublished on most of them. What the money does buy, if you pick by your own priority — 5G, charging speed, camera or the lowest price — is a phone that handles everyday life without a serious flaw. Before paying, confirm the unit is PTA approved and keep the warranty card; if your current phone still works but struggles, our fixes for a phone that has slowed down and a battery that drains too fast may save you the purchase entirely.

Charging is very slow

It used to fill up in 1 hour, now it takes 3? In 90% of cases the problem is the charger or cable — not the phone. Follow this checklist.

Steps

  1. Use the original charger and cable
    The charger in the phone's box is built for its watt rating. Cheap market cables are the biggest cause of slow charging — get a branded cable.
  2. Clean the charging port
    Turn the phone OFF. Carefully remove lint and dust from the charge port with a toothpick or soft brush. Often the phone picks up 'fast charging' again right after.
  3. Check the charging speed
    When you plug in, the lock screen should say 'Fast charging' / 'Rapid charging' instead of just 'Charging'. If it only says 'Charging', change the cable/charger.
  4. Stop heavy use while charging
    If the phone keeps running games/videos while plugged in, it uses almost as much battery as it gains — charging seems stuck. Just leave the phone aside while it charges.
  5. Software update and battery saver
    Battery saver mode slows the charge rate — turn it OFF while charging. Keep the software updated too.
  6. Battery health check
    Everything fine but still slow? The battery may be old. Get a battery capacity test at a service center.

Tips

  • Charging from a laptop USB port is always slow — use a wall adapter.
  • If the phone hits 0%, turn it OFF and charge for 30 minutes, then turn it ON — it helps kick-start the charge.
Warning: Fake 'fast charger' brands can overheat and damage the battery. Only use well-known/brand chargers.

Our Verdict

If your phone used to charge in an hour and now takes three, the phone itself is rarely the culprit. In most cases the fix is boring and cheap: the original charger, a decent cable and a charging port cleaned of pocket lint solve the problem. Work through the checklist above in order — charger and cable first, the port second, settings third — before spending money on repairs. Only when every step fails does the battery itself become the prime suspect, and by then a capacity test at a service center will confirm it within minutes. The one thing not to do is buy an unbranded 'fast charger' from a market stall: it can overheat and shorten your battery's life.

Do's & Don'ts

Do this

  • Use the charger and cable that came with the phone, or a branded replacement
  • Clean the charging port gently, with the phone switched off
  • Watch for 'Fast charging' on the lock screen — it confirms full speed
  • Turn battery saver off while the phone is charging
  • Charge from a wall adapter rather than a laptop

Avoid this

  • Cheap unbranded cables — the most common cause of slow charging
  • Gaming or watching video while the phone is plugged in
  • Fake 'fast chargers' that can overheat and damage the battery
  • Charging from a laptop USB port when you are in a hurry
  • Ignoring a loose, wobbly cable connection — usually lint or a worn cable

FAQs

Why did my phone suddenly start charging slowly?

The most common causes are a worn or low-quality cable, a charger that is not the phone's original, and lint packed into the charging port. Work through the checklist on this page in order — in about 90% of cases the problem is the charger or the cable, not the phone.

Does battery saver mode slow down charging?

Yes. Battery saver deliberately reduces the charging rate, so turn it off while the phone is plugged in if you want full speed. Keeping the phone's software updated also helps, because updates can fix charging-related bugs.

Why is charging from a laptop so slow?

A laptop USB port delivers far less power than a wall adapter — it is designed for data and small accessories, not for fast charging. Always use a wall adapter with the phone's own charger when you need speed.

Are cheap 'fast chargers' dangerous?

They can be. Fake or unbranded fast chargers can overheat and damage the battery over time. Stick to the charger that came in the box or a well-known brand, even if it costs a little more.

When should I get my phone battery replaced?

If the original charger, a clean port and the correct settings still leave charging slow, the battery itself may be old and worn. A service center can run a battery capacity test and confirm whether a replacement is worth the money.

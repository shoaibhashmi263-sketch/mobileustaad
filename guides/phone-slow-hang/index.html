Phone has become slow / hangs

Old phone or new, these steps help speed up almost every Android phone.

Why phones slow down over time

A phone rarely slows down simply because it is 'old' — it slows down because of what piles up on it. Apps grow bigger with every update and run more background services, storage fills up until the phone has almost no room left to work, and years of photos, videos and app data make every search and gallery load slower. Software updates written for newer, faster models can also feel heavy on an older phone. The encouraging part is that most of the slowdown is this pile-up, not dying hardware — and the steps below clear it in a logical order: quick wins first, and the factory reset only if everything else fails.

Steps

  1. Restart the phone
    Simple but powerful — restarting once a week clears the memory that causes hangs.
  2. Delete apps you don't need
    Uninstall apps you haven't opened in months (Settings > Apps). Fewer apps = faster phone.
  3. Speed up animations (Developer Options)
    Tap Build Number 7 times in Settings > About Phone, then set Window/Transition animation scale from 1x to 0.5x in Developer Options. The phone will instantly feel faster.
  4. Keep 20% of storage free
    When storage is 90%+ full, the phone hangs. Empty the gallery trash and move/delete old videos.
  5. Remove heavy launchers/themes
    Fancy launchers and live wallpapers eat RAM. Use a simple launcher and a static wallpaper.
  6. Factory reset: last resort
    If nothing works, first back up WhatsApp and your photos, then go to Settings > System > Reset > Factory Reset. It makes the phone feel like day one.
  7. Clear the cache of heavy apps
    Social, video and browser apps collect hundreds of MBs of cache over time. Settings > Apps > [app] > Storage > Clear Cache frees that space without deleting your data or logging you out.
  8. Stop apps starting on their own
    Many phones have an 'Auto-start' or 'App launch' setting (under Settings > Apps or Battery). Stop apps you rarely use from launching themselves — fewer background apps means more free memory for the app actually in your hand.
  9. Install pending updates
    Performance improvements and bug fixes arrive through updates. Install pending system updates, and update your most-used apps from the Play Store.
  10. Check the battery's condition
    On some phones, a badly worn battery makes the system limit performance to avoid sudden shutdowns. If your phone is slow AND the battery drains fast or the percentage jumps around, the battery itself may be the real problem.

If the phone hangs or freezes completely

A phone that freezes on one screen and ignores all touches needs a force restart: hold the Power button — on some models Power plus Volume Down — for about ten seconds, until the logo appears. Your photos and data stay safe; only the stuck session ends. If the freezes return, notice the pattern: if the phone always hangs when a particular app opens, that app is the culprit, so clear its cache and data or uninstall it. If it freezes during calls or while doing nothing at all, the cause is more likely the system or the hardware, and the pattern you noticed will help a technician later.

When to visit a service centre

If the phone is still slow after a factory reset with only a few basic apps installed, the cause is probably hardware — worn storage or a failing battery on an older phone. Also get help if it restarts by itself, the touchscreen stops responding in patches, or it gets hot during light use. A technician can test the battery and storage health and tell you honestly whether a repair is worth paying for, or whether the phone has simply reached the end of its useful life.

Make the speed last

  • Review your apps every few months and delete the ones you no longer open.
  • Move photos and videos to a computer or cloud backup regularly, instead of letting storage sit above 90% full.
  • On a low-memory phone, prefer the 'Lite' versions of heavy apps — they are built to run lighter.
  • Restart the phone weekly; it is the simplest maintenance there is, and it prevents most slow build-ups.

Tips

  • Installing apps on the SD card slows the phone down — keep them on internal storage.
  • Junk cleaner apps often become bloatware themselves; the built-in Storage Cleaner is enough.

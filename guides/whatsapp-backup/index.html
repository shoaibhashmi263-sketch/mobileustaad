WhatsApp backup won't restore

Getting a new phone and finding your WhatsApp chat history missing is stressful. These steps work in 90% of cases.

Steps

  1. Check the same phone number and same account
    Use the same SIM number on the new phone as on the old one, and make sure you're logged in with the Google account the backup was made on (check the account name in Settings > Google).
  2. Confirm the backup on the old phone
    Is the old phone still working? Go to WhatsApp > Settings > Chats > Chat Backup and check the 'Last Backup' date. If no backup was ever made, there is nothing to restore. Tap 'Back Up' and create a Google Drive backup.
  3. Restore on strong Wi-Fi
    Restore on Wi-Fi only — a large backup can stall or stay incomplete on mobile data. Install WhatsApp, verify your number, tap 'Restore', and keep the phone on-screen (don't lock it).
  4. Keep free space on the phone
    Restoring needs twice the backup size in free phone storage. If storage is full, delete some files and try again.
  5. Check Google Drive space
    Check drive.google.com to see if there is space left in Drive. A full Drive can block both creating and restoring backups.
  6. If no backup exists
    If no backup was ever made on the old phone, the chats can't come back — but for the future, turn on Settings > Chats > Chat Backup > End-to-end encrypted backup and select 'Daily' backup so this doesn't happen again.

Tips

  • Never factory-reset the old phone without verifying the backup first.
  • For large video backups: keep 'Include videos' off so the backup stays small and fast.

Restore stuck, failing, or taking forever?

  1. Update everything first
    Update WhatsApp from the Play Store or App Store, and on Android also update Google Play Services. An outdated app often fails in the middle of a restore for no visible reason.
  2. Let the media restore finish in the background
    Chats come back first; photos and videos keep downloading afterwards and can look 'missing' for a while. Keep WhatsApp open on Wi-Fi with the charger plugged in, and give a large backup real time — leaving it overnight is normal for very big backups.
  3. Clear only the cache
    On Android: Settings > Apps > WhatsApp > Storage > Clear Cache, then try again. Never tap 'Clear Data' at this stage — that wipes the app's local state and forces a completely fresh setup.
  4. Double-check which account holds the backup
    The most common hidden cause of 'no backup found' is a second Google account. On drive.google.com, open the Backups section and confirm a WhatsApp backup exists there, then make sure the phone and WhatsApp are using that same account.
  5. Turn off VPN and battery restrictions
    A VPN or an aggressive battery saver can quietly block the download. Disable the VPN, set WhatsApp's battery usage to Unrestricted during the restore, and keep the screen awake while it runs.

Moving between Android and iPhone

Google Drive backups and iCloud backups are separate worlds — an Android (Drive) backup cannot be restored directly on an iPhone, and an iPhone (iCloud) backup cannot be restored on Android. When switching platforms, use the official transfer path instead: WhatsApp's own chat transfer offered during setup (on a new iPhone, the 'Move to iOS' flow; on a new Android phone, the transfer option shown while setting up). Start the transfer before you begin using WhatsApp on the new phone — once you have registered and skipped the restore prompt, the old history cannot be merged in afterwards without reinstalling the app.

Three traps that lose chats forever

  • Tapping 'Skip' at the restore prompt: There is no 'restore later' option inside WhatsApp afterwards. If you skipped it, the only way back is to uninstall and reinstall, then restore during setup.
  • Forgetting the encrypted-backup password: If you turned on end-to-end encrypted backup and forget the password (or lose the 64-digit key), not even WhatsApp can recover that backup. Store it in a password manager.
  • Wiping the old phone too early: Keep the old phone untouched until the new phone shows your full chat list with its media. The old phone's local backup is your only second chance if the cloud restore fails.

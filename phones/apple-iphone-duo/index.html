Overview

The iPhone Duo is Apple's first foldable phone, announced in September 2026: a book-style foldable with a 7.6-inch 120Hz inner OLED and a 5.4-inch cover display. It runs the 2nm A20 Pro with 12GB of RAM, dual 48MP rear cameras with 2x optical zoom, a 12MP cover camera plus an under-display inner camera, and side-mounted Touch ID instead of Face ID. The grade-5 titanium body is IP68 rated and weighs 254g, in Star White and Night Sky. Pre-orders opened October 16, 2026 with first sales on October 23; Pakistan is not in the first wave, so no PK price is published.

Gaming review

The A20 Pro is flagship silicon, so every game runs at its best — but the foldable form factor and price make this a luxury device first, a gaming phone second. (Editorial estimate, not a measured benchmark.)

Our Verdict

The Apple iPhone Duo is one of Apple's premium offerings, led by smooth 120Hz Super Retina XDR foldable OLED, 120Hz ProMotion (nano-texture); 5.4-inch cover OLED display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the Apple A20 Pro (2nm) chipset and a 4,883mAh battery with 60W charging. On the software side it runs iOS 27. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

FAQs

What is the Apple iPhone Duo price in Pakistan?

The Apple iPhone Duo is available in Pakistan at Rs 744,999 (market price, source: WhatMobile (expected price, 'Coming Soon', opened 22 Oct 2026), checked 2026-10-22). Prices can differ slightly between cities and shops, so confirm before you buy.

Is the Apple iPhone Duo PTA approved?

The Apple iPhone Duo is largely sold through market and import channels in Pakistan, so PTA status depends on the individual unit. Before paying, verify the IMEI via DIRBS (send the IMEI to 8484) and factor in the PTA tax if the unit is not already approved.

How is the Apple iPhone Duo battery life?

The Apple iPhone Duo carries a 4,883mAh battery with 60W fast charging. Most users should get through a typical day of calls, social media and video; heavy gaming will drain it faster.

How good is the Apple iPhone Duo camera?

The rear setup is 48 MP Dual Fusion main + 48 MP ultrawide (2x optical), and the front camera is 12MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Apple A20 Pro (2nm).

How long will the Apple iPhone Duo get software updates?

Apple has not published an update promise for this model. Apple does not promise a fixed number of iOS upgrades, but guarantees at least 5 years of security updates — in practice iPhones stay supported much longer.

Is the Apple iPhone Duo good for gaming?

The Apple A20 Pro (2nm) earns a "Excellent" rating in our gaming assessment (score 4/5). Popular titles like PUBG Mobile and Call of Duty: Mobile are playable at settings that match the chipset's class; sustained heavy gaming will warm the phone up, as with most phones in this range.

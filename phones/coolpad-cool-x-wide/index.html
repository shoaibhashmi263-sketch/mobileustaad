Overview

The Coolpad Cool X Wide is Coolpad's phone, released in 2026. Its 6.38-inch HD+ TFT LCD (1158x720, wide 16:10 aspect ratio) display runs at 90Hz, a step up in smoothness from the basic 60Hz panels still common at this price.

Power comes from the MediaTek Helio G99 (4G LTE only), which is aimed at everyday use — calls, social media, video and light gaming all run comfortably. The rear camera setup is 50MP main + macro (second sensor details unconfirmed), and the high-resolution main sensor is the one you will lean on for daylight shots, which come out detailed. Battery capacity is 4,600mAh, and 18W charging means top-ups are reasonably brisk when you are in a hurry.

On the software side it runs Android 16. It is aimed at buyers stepping up from an older phone who want a modern, balanced package. A confirmed Pakistan price has not been published yet, so we do not list one rather than guess; imported units may show up in local markets first.

Gaming review

The Helio G99 handles casual and mid-tier games acceptably, but the wide low-res LCD and entry positioning make this a media-consumption device, not a gaming phone.

Overview

The Google Pixel 8 is Google's premium mid-range phone, released in 2023. Its 6.2-inch OLED display runs at 120Hz, so scrolling, animations and supported games feel noticeably smoother than on a standard 60Hz panel.

Power comes from the Google Tensor G3, which drives the phone's everyday performance, working alongside the RAM and storage on offer. The rear camera setup is 50 MP wide + 12 MP ultrawide, and the high-resolution main sensor is the one you will lean on for daylight shots, which come out detailed. Battery capacity is 4,575mAh, and 27W charging means top-ups are reasonably brisk when you are in a hurry.

It is aimed at buyers who want premium features without paying full flagship money. In Pakistan it is listed at Rs 85,000 (market price, checked 2026-10-05) — confirm the final price, variant and warranty with the seller before paying.

Our Verdict

The Google Pixel 8 is a premium mid-range phone from Google, and its strongest suit is clear: smooth 120Hz OLED display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the Google Tensor G3 chipset and a 4,575mAh battery with 27W charging. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

FAQs

What is the Google Pixel 8 price in Pakistan?

The Google Pixel 8 is available in Pakistan at Rs 85,000 (market price, source: OLX Pakistan listings, checked 2026-10-05). Prices can differ slightly between cities and shops, so confirm before you buy.

Is the Google Pixel 8 PTA approved?

The Google Pixel 8 is largely sold through market and import channels in Pakistan, so PTA status depends on the individual unit. Before paying, verify the IMEI via DIRBS (send the IMEI to 8484) and factor in the PTA tax if the unit is not already approved.

How is the Google Pixel 8 battery life?

The Google Pixel 8 carries a 4,575mAh battery with 27W fast charging. Most users should get through a typical day of calls, social media and video; heavy gaming will drain it faster.

How good is the Google Pixel 8 camera?

The rear setup is 50 MP wide + 12 MP ultrawide, and the front camera is 10MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Google Tensor G3.

How long will the Google Pixel 8 get software updates?

Google promises 7 years of Android updates and 7 years of security patches for this model.

What are the Google Pixel 8 variants in Pakistan?

The variant tracked in Pakistan is 8GB RAM with 128GB storage. If a shop offers a different variant, it is likely an imported unit — confirm the warranty and PTA status for that specific unit.

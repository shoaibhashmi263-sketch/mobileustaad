The Google Pixel 9 Pro XL is a flagship-grade phone that leads with smooth 120Hz OLED display that feels fast in everyday use. FAQs

What is the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL price in Pakistan?

The Google Pixel 9 Pro XL is available in Pakistan at Rs 297,000 (market price, source: DealDone.pk market data, checked 2026-10-05). Prices can differ slightly between cities and shops, so confirm before you buy.

Is the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL PTA approved?

The Google Pixel 9 Pro XL is largely sold through market and import channels in Pakistan, so PTA status depends on the individual unit. Before paying, verify the IMEI via DIRBS (send the IMEI to 8484) and factor in the PTA tax if the unit is not already approved.

How is the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL battery life?

The Google Pixel 9 Pro XL carries a 5,060mAh battery with 37W fast charging. That is a large cell for this class, and most users should comfortably get a full day — heavier users with long gaming or video sessions may still charge overnight.

How good is the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL camera?

The rear setup is 50 MP wide + 48 MP telephoto (5x) + 48 MP ultrawide, and the front camera is 42MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Google Tensor G4.

How long will the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL get software updates?

Google promises 7 years of Android updates and 7 years of security patches for this model.

What are the Google Pixel 9 Pro XL variants in Pakistan?

The variant tracked in Pakistan is 16GB RAM with 128GB storage. If a shop offers a different variant, it is likely an imported unit — confirm the warranty and PTA status for that specific unit.

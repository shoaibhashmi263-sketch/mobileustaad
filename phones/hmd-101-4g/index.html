Overview

There is no camera, no apps and no touchscreen on the HMD 101 4G — and for its buyers, that is the whole appeal. This is a calls-and-texts phone with a 2.0-inch screen, a physical keypad and a 1,000mAh battery that lasts for days between charges instead of hours. It runs HMD's simple S30+ software, supports 4G calling, and costs just Rs 6,975 in Pakistan.

It suits older users who find smartphones confusing, workers who need a tough second phone for site use, and anyone doing a deliberate digital detox. The HMD 102 4G adds a small camera; the 101 does not bother — it just makes calls, sends texts and keeps going.

Gaming review

It's a basic 4G feature phone, not built for gaming.

Our Verdict

The HMD 101 4G is a budget phone that leads with large 2.0-inch TFT display. It packs the Unisoc 8910 chipset and a 1,000mAh battery with 2.75W charging. On the software side it runs S30+ (feature phone RTOS). Buy it if the strengths above match how you use a phone; look elsewhere if this matters to you: 60hz display — scrolling feels less smooth than 120hz rivals.

Overview

The HMD Crest was HMD's first self-branded smartphone for India, an affordable 5G phone built around the 6nm Unisoc T760 chip. It stands out with a 6.67-inch FHD+ OLED display and a 50MP front camera with gesture-triggered hands-free selfies, backed by a 5000mAh battery with 33W charging. Like other HMD phones it supports user repairs via iFixit parts.

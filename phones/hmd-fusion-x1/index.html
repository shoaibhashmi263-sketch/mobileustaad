Overview

The HMD Fusion X1 is a teen-focused variant of the modular Fusion, unveiled at MWC 2025 with the Xplora parental-control platform pre-installed for location tracking, app approvals and screen-time management. Hardware is nearly identical to the Fusion — Snapdragon 4 Gen 2, 108MP camera, 5000mAh battery — including support for the Fusion accessory outfits. An FC Barcelona edition was announced alongside it.

Our Verdict

The HMD Fusion X1 is a phone that leads with large 6.56-inch IPS LCD display. It packs the Qualcomm Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 (4 nm) chipset and a 5,000mAh battery with 33W charging. Buy it if the strengths above match how you use a phone; look elsewhere if this matters to you: entry-level chipset — fine for daily use, not for heavy gaming.

How is the HMD Fusion X1 battery life?

The HMD Fusion X1 carries a 5,000mAh battery with 33W fast charging. That is a large cell for this class, and most users should comfortably get a full day — heavier users with long gaming or video sessions may still charge overnight.

How good is the HMD Fusion X1 camera?

The rear setup is 108MP wide (EIS) + 2MP depth (1080p), and the front camera is 50MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Qualcomm Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 (4 nm).

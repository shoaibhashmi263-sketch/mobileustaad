Overview

The HMD Fusion is a modular 5G smartphone with interchangeable 'Smart Outfits' — covers that attach via smart pins to add features like a selfie ring light, a gaming controller or extra battery, and can even be 3D-printed via HMD's open toolkit. Underneath it is a Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 phone with a 108MP main camera, 50MP selfie camera and 5000mAh battery. The semi-transparent shell is fully repairable with iFixit parts promised for seven years.

Gaming review

Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 — casual gaming only; the gaming-controller outfit is a fun extra, not a powerhouse.

Overview

The HMD Rise launched in India on 26 November 2026 as a budget 4G phone, going on sale through Flipkart from 2 December at a Day 1 special price of INR 9,999 (about $108) for its single 4GB/64GB configuration. It is aimed at first-time smartphone buyers, with a dual-tone design in Indigo Silver, Midnight Gold and Emerald Gold, and Indus by Sarvam AI, an assistant that supports 22 Indian languages. The phone has a 6.52-inch HD+ IPS LCD with a waterdrop notch, a Unisoc SC9863A chip, 4GB of RAM and 64GB of storage expandable to 128GB by microSD. Photography is a 13MP dual rear camera with LED flash and a 5MP front camera. The 5,000mAh battery charges at 10W over USB-C, and the retail box includes the charger. It runs Android 16 Go edition with two years of quarterly security updates, and includes a 3.5mm headphone jack, FM radio and IP52 splash protection. No Pakistan launch or price has been announced, so the listed price is an expected price converted from the India launch price.

Gaming review

The Unisoc SC9863A is a very basic chip — only light apps and casual games at low settings.

Our Verdict

The HMD Rise is a budget phone that leads with big 6.52-inch IPS LCD (576x1280, 60Hz) screen for video and reading. It packs the Unisoc SC9863A chipset and a 5,000mAh battery with 10W charging. Software is Android 16 (Go edition) as listed by HMD. Shortlist it if the spec mix suits you — provided this does not put you off: display refresh rate is only 60hz.

Overview

The HMD Skyline is HMD's flagship-tier phone with a Lumia-inspired design, built around the Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 and a 6.55-inch 144Hz P-OLED display. Its triple camera system (108MP OIS main, 50MP 2x telephoto, 13MP ultrawide) and 50MP autofocus selfie camera make it HMD's strongest camera phone. It supports 33W wired, 15W Qi2 magnetic wireless and 5W reverse wireless charging, with a user-replaceable battery and iFixit repair kits.

Gaming review

Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 + 144Hz display — the best gaming phone HMD ever made; PUBG on high settings smooth.

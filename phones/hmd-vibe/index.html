Overview

HMD's Vibe is a US-focused model with no Pakistan listing found, and the Rs 33,480 figure in the record comes from PhoneAqua as a market reference rather than a local launch price. That alone makes it an import curiosity rather than a mainstream option here. The hardware is basic: 3GB of RAM, 64GB of storage, a headphone jack, no NFC, no 5G and no fingerprint sensor.

The 6.56-inch IPS LCD runs at 90Hz but only at 720x1480 resolution, so while scrolling looks smoother than on a basic screen, outright sharpness is limited. A Snapdragon 680 4G chipset powers the phone and gaming is rated average, though the notes are clear that it is not a gaming phone: 3D titles struggle, and 2D casual games and simple puzzlers are the realistic limit. It runs Android 14, and the record publishes no update support for the model, so software longevity is an unknown.

Photography comes from a 13MP wide rear camera with an auxiliary lens, capable of 1080p video, plus a 5MP front camera; it is a minimal arrangement with no ultrawide, telephoto or stabilisation mentioned. The 4,000mAh battery is smaller than the 5,000mAh norm in this class, and 10W charging is slow, so both capacity and refilling speed trail most rivals. Only consider it if it appears at a very low import price and your needs are calls, messaging and light apps; for buyers in Pakistan, locally sold phones offer more battery, more memory and proper availability for similar money.

Our Verdict

The HMD Vibe is a budget phone from HMD, and its strongest suit is clear: roomy 6.56-inch IPS LCD panel. It packs the Qualcomm Snapdragon 680 4G (6 nm) chipset and a 4,000mAh battery with 10W charging. It is one of the more rounded options in its bracket — just go in knowing its main trade-off: charging is slow at 10w.

Overview

The Honor HONOR 600S is Honor's phone, released in 2026. Its 6.87-inch IPS TFT LCD (1592x720 HD+, 120Hz, ~1020 nits peak, DC dimming, wet-hand touch) display runs at 120Hz, so scrolling, animations and supported games feel noticeably smoother than on a standard 60Hz panel.

Power comes from the Qualcomm Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 (4nm; octa-core 2 performance + 6 efficiency cores, Adreno 613), which is aimed at everyday use — calls, social media, video and light gaming all run comfortably. The rear camera setup is 50 MP main (f/1.8) + auxiliary depth sensor; 8x digital zoom, no OIS; 1080p video, and the high-resolution main sensor is the one you will lean on for daylight shots, which come out detailed. Battery capacity is 8,100mAh — well above average, and the kind of size that can stretch to two days for lighter users, and 45W charging means top-ups are genuinely quick when you are in a hurry.

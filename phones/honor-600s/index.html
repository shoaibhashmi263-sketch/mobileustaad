The HONOR 600S is a rugged mid-range phone launched in Vietnam: a 6.87-inch 120Hz display, the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4, a 50MP main camera, an 8,100mAh battery with 45W HONOR SuperCharge, IP68/IP69/IP69K durability with 2.5m drop certification, and a dedicated AI button.

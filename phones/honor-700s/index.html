Overview

The Honor 700S launched in China on 23 November 2026 as the entry model of the Honor 700 series, keeping the series' two headline features: a 1.59-inch OLED display on the back and a 9,000mAh battery. Its 6.57-inch LTPS AMOLED front display runs at 120Hz with a 2728x1264 resolution and up to 8,000 nits of peak brightness. Photography is led by a 200MP main camera with optical stabilisation, joined by a 12MP ultrawide/macro lens, while a 50MP front camera handles selfies. The 9,000mAh silicon-carbon battery charges at 80W wired. Unlike the Honor 700 and 700 Pro, the 700S has no periscope telephoto camera, no wireless charging and no heart-rate sensing on the rear display. It runs MagicOS 11 on Android 17 in a 7.96mm body rated IP66, IP68, IP69 and IP69K, with an in-display fingerprint reader, Wi-Fi 6 and stereo speakers. The China price starts at CNY 3,599 for 12GB/256GB; no Pakistan launch or price has been announced, so the listed price is an expected price converted from the China launch price.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 with 12GB of RAM handles popular games smoothly at medium to high settings on the 120Hz display, and the 9,000mAh battery keeps long sessions going.

Our Verdict

Positioned as a premium mid-range phone, the Honor 700S puts 120Hz LTPS AMOLED (2728x1264, 120Hz, 3840Hz PWM, HDR Vivid, 8000 nits peak) panel keeps scrolling and animations fluid at the top of its pitch. It packs the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 (4nm) chipset and a 9,000mAh battery with 80W charging. Software is Android 17 as listed by Honor. As a package it holds together well for the money — with its main drawback understood upfront: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

Overview

The Honor Magic9 is a compact flagship built around a serious camera story: dual 200MP ARRI co-developed sensors — a 1/1.4-inch main and a 3.5x periscope telephoto — with cinematic LogC3 video tools. A 6.37-inch 120Hz OLED, Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, 3D ultrasonic fingerprint sensor, and a large 8,000mAh silicon-carbon battery with 80W wired plus 50W wireless charging round out a China-only launch package starting at CNY 4,999.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 is last year's top Qualcomm chip but still firmly flagship-class — high-refresh 120Hz gaming at max settings is no problem, backed by 12-16GB LPDDR5X RAM and Honor's Phantom Engine 3.0.

Overview

The Honor Win RT 2 launched in China on October 21, 2026 as the battery king of the Win 2 gaming series. It keeps the 6.89-inch 2K 185Hz flat OLED of the flagship Win 2 but swaps in the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 and a record 12,070mAh battery with 90W wired + 27W reverse charging. The camera is a 200MP Samsung HP5 main with OIS plus a 12MP ultrawide, and the phone packs active cooling, a matte metal frame with WIN energy ring, dual 1216 speakers, 3D ultrasonic in-display fingerprint and IP68/IP69/IP69K ratings on Android 17. Colors: Quick Black, Designated Win, Red-Blue Buff.

Gaming review

Flagship gaming hardware: Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, active cooling, a 185Hz 2K OLED with 5,000Hz touch sampling and 21ms tap latency, WIN Display/Touch chips and an 800Hz gyroscope. The 12,070mAh battery is the largest ever in a mainstream phone. (Editorial estimate, not a measured benchmark.)

Price notes

On sale in China from Oct 21, 2026 (national subsidy pricing from CNY 3,149). Variants: 12/256, 16/256, 12/512, 16/512. 8.5mm thick, 238g. No global/Pakistan launch announced. Sources: gizmochina, gsmdome, mymobileindia, huaweicentral (Oct 21, 2026).

Our Verdict

The Honor Win RT 2 is a phone from Honor, and its strongest suit is clear: smooth 185Hz 2K LTPS OLED, flat (Visionox, 5,920Hz PWM dimming, 5,000Hz touch sampling, 0.93mm bezels) display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 (3nm) chipset and a 12,070mAh battery with 90W charging. On the software side it runs Android 17 (MagicOS 11). It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

How is the Honor Win RT 2 battery life?

The Honor Win RT 2 carries a 12,070mAh battery with 90W fast charging. That is a large cell for this class, and most users should comfortably get a full day — heavier users with long gaming or video sessions may still charge overnight.

How good is the Honor Win RT 2 camera?

The rear setup is 200MP main (Samsung HP5, 1/1.55-inch, OIS) + 12MP ultrawide (no telephoto), and the front camera is 50MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 (3nm).

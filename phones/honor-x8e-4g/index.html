Overview

The Honor X8e 4G appeared quietly on Honor's Azerbaijan website around 28 October 2026 as a durability-first entry-level phone, nearly identical to the Honor X7e Plus 4G from August. Its headline features are a 108MP main camera — unusual at this price — and a 7,700mAh battery, wrapped in a body rated IP68, IP69 and IP69K with SGS 5-star impact resistance from 2.5-metre drops. The 6.87-inch TFT LCD runs at 120Hz with 1,020 nits of peak brightness and a punch-hole 5MP selfie camera, while the Snapdragon 685 chipset keeps it strictly 4G-only and caps video recording at 1080p. The 7,700mAh battery charges at 45W Honor SuperCharge, and the phone is built for harsh conditions: it works from -30°C to +50°C, survives a day in humid, salty air, and offers an underwater photo mode plus a wet-touch display. On the software side it runs Android 16 with MagicOS 10, including Honor's AI button, Circle to Search and RAM Turbo. It is aimed at buyers who want a tough, long-lasting budget phone rather than speed or 5G. No Pakistan price or launch has been announced yet — Azerbaijani retailers list the sole 6/256GB variant at AZN 688-820, with no official MSRP published. Colours: Sunrise Orange, Desert Gold and Velvet Black.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 685 with a 120Hz display handles light games, but this is an entry-level 4G chip — demanding titles will struggle, and the phone's strengths are battery and durability, not gaming.

Overview

The Honor X9e Pro launched globally on 24 September 2026 and reached open sale in the UAE on 30 October as the first Pro model in Honor's durability-focused X series. Its headline is an 11,000mAh silicon-carbon battery that Honor rates for up to three days of mixed use, wrapped in a body with IP68/IP69/IP69K ratings, SGS 5-star drop resistance to 3 metres and a 6.8-inch 1.5K AMOLED with a claimed 10,000-nit peak brightness. Photography comes from a 108MP main camera with OIS, joined by a 5MP ultrawide, while a 16MP front camera handles selfies. The 11,000mAh battery charges at 80W wired and can reverse-charge accessories at 27W. Stereo speakers, an in-display optical fingerprint sensor, Wi-Fi 6 and NFC complete the package. On the software side it runs MagicOS 10 on Android 16. It is aimed at buyers who want extreme battery life and toughness in a midrange phone rather than flagship speed. In Pakistan it is on pre-booking / retail from November 2026 at Rs 119,999 for 8GB/256GB (WhatMobile distributor retail; a competing Rs 124,999 figure is also listed by other aggregators). In the UAE it costs AED 1,499 for 8GB/256GB and AED 1,799 for 12GB/256GB. Colours: Dawn Gold, Midnight Black, Reddish Brown, Royal Purple.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 with a 120Hz AMOLED handles everyday games well and most demanding titles at reduced settings — this is a battery-and-durability phone first, not a gaming flagship.

Our Verdict

The Honor X9e Pro arrives as a premium mid-range phone whose headline strength is 120Hz AMOLED, 1.5K (1280x2788, 120Hz, 10000 nits peak, 3840Hz PWM) panel keeps scrolling and animations fluid. Headline hardware: the Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 (4nm) chipset and a 11,000mAh battery with 80W charging. Software is Android 16 (MagicOS 10) as listed by Honor. It is one of the more rounded options in its bracket — provided this does not put you off: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

The Honor X9e Pro carries a 11,000mAh battery with 80W fast charging. It is one of the larger batteries you will find at this level — most users will see a full day, helped by 80W charging when a top-up is needed.

The rear camera system is 108MP main (f/1.75, OIS) + 5MP ultrawide (f/2.2); selfies use a 16MP front camera. That is a respectable camera specification for the segment, expect its best results in daylight; night output depends on the Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 (4nm)'s image processing.

Overview

The Huawei Enjoy 100 Pro Max launched in China on 28 October 2026 as the top model of the Enjoy 100 lineup. Its headline feature is an 8,800mAh Giant Whale battery — the largest in the series — which Huawei rates for up to 49 hours of calls or 29.5 hours of online video, with two-way BeiDou satellite messaging for areas without coverage. The 6.84-inch OLED display runs at 120Hz with a claimed peak brightness of up to 8,000 nits, and the Kirin 8020A or Kirin 8020 chipset (depending on storage) runs HarmonyOS 7 with Huawei's anti-fraud suite, including AI deepfake face detection. Photography comes from a 50MP RYYB main camera plus an 8MP selfie camera, while the reinforced body carries dual five-star drop-resistance certification with an IP65 rating. On the software side it runs HarmonyOS 7. It is aimed at buyers who want the biggest battery and brightest screen in Huawei's mid-range lineup. No Pakistan price or launch has been announced yet — the China prices start at CNY 1,999 (128GB), rising to CNY 2,899 (512GB). Colours: Obsidian Black, Snow White, Azure Sea and Quicksand Gold.

Gaming review

The Kirin 8020-series chip with a 120Hz OLED display and an 8,800mAh battery handles long gaming sessions well, but this is a battery-first phone, not a gaming phone — there is no gaming chip or active cooling.

Our Verdict

The Huawei Enjoy 100 Pro Max is a phone that leads with smooth 120Hz OLED (1272x2756, 120Hz, up to 8,000 nits peak) display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the HiSilicon Kirin 8020A (128/256GB) / Kirin 8020 (512GB) chipset and a 8,800mAh battery with 40W charging. On the software side it runs HarmonyOS 7. Buy it if the strengths above match how you use a phone; look elsewhere if this matters to you: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

The Huawei Enjoy 100 Pro Max carries a 8,800mAh battery with 40W fast charging. That is a large cell for this class, and most users should comfortably get a full day — heavier users with long gaming or video sessions may still charge overnight.

How good is the Huawei Enjoy 100 Pro Max camera?

The rear setup is 50MP main (RYYB, autofocus), and the front camera is 8MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the HiSilicon Kirin 8020A (128/256GB) / Kirin 8020 (512GB).

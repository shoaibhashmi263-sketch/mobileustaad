Overview

The Huawei Enjoy 100 Pro launched in China on 28 October 2026 as the middle model of the Enjoy 100 lineup. Its headline feature is a 50MP RYYB main camera — a sensor design that gathers more light than a standard Bayer sensor — paired with a 32MP selfie camera, in a slim 192g body with a floral-patterned back. The 6.7-inch OLED display runs at 120Hz behind Kunlun Glass, and the Kirin 8020A chipset, which Huawei says is 53% faster than the Enjoy 90 Plus chip, keeps HarmonyOS 7 smooth with the Ark Engine. The 6,100mAh battery supports 40W fast charging, and the Xuanjia body architecture carries dual five-star drop-resistance certification with an IP65 rating. On the software side it runs HarmonyOS 7 with Huawei's anti-fraud call and web protection. It is aimed at buyers who want a strong main camera and a light, tough phone without paying flagship prices. No Pakistan price or launch has been announced yet — the China prices start at CNY 1,799 (128GB), rising to CNY 2,699 (512GB). Colours: Peacock Blue, Feather Purple, Feather Sand White and Phantom Night Black.

Gaming review

The Kirin 8020A with a 120Hz OLED display handles popular titles at sensible settings, but this is a camera-and-durability phone, not a gaming phone — there is no gaming chip or active cooling.

Our Verdict

The Huawei Enjoy 100 Pro is a phone from Huawei, and its strongest suit is clear: smooth 120Hz OLED (1084x2412, 120Hz, Kunlun Glass) display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the HiSilicon Kirin 8020A chipset and a 6,100mAh battery with 40W charging. On the software side it runs HarmonyOS 7. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

How is the Huawei Enjoy 100 Pro battery life?

The Huawei Enjoy 100 Pro carries a 6,100mAh battery with 40W fast charging. That is a large cell for this class, and most users should comfortably get a full day — heavier users with long gaming or video sessions may still charge overnight.

How good is the Huawei Enjoy 100 Pro camera?

The rear setup is 50MP main (RYYB, autofocus), and the front camera is 32MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the HiSilicon Kirin 8020A.

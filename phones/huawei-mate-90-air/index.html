Overview

The Huawei Mate 90 Air launched in China on 24 November 2026 as the thinnest Mate bar phone yet, just 6.58mm thick with a 7-inch 1.5K quad-curved LTPO OLED display running at 120Hz with up to 4,000 nits of peak brightness. It runs on Huawei's Kirin 9020 chip with 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage, and the 6,500mAh silicon-carbon battery supports 66W wired charging. Photography is led by a 50MP main camera with optical stabilisation, joined by a 12MP 3x telephoto with OIS, an 8MP ultrawide that doubles as a macro lens, and Huawei's Red Maple multispectral colour camera, while a 10.7MP front camera handles selfies. The phone supports Wi-Fi 7+, NearLink and two-way BeiDou satellite messaging in China. It ships with HarmonyOS 7 in a 208g body rated IP68/IP69, with a side-mounted fingerprint reader, NFC and stereo speakers. The China price starts at CNY 4,999 for 12GB/256GB; no Pakistan launch or price has been announced, so the listed price is an expected price converted from the China launch price.

Gaming review

The Kirin 9020 with 12GB of RAM and a 120Hz display handles demanding 3D games at high settings, and the 6,500mAh battery keeps long sessions going without a recharge.

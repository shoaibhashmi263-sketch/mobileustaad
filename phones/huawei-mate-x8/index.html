Overview

The Huawei Mate X8 launched in China on 24 November 2026 as Huawei's most powerful foldable yet, built around an 8.2-inch foldable LTPO OLED inner display with a 1-120Hz refresh rate and up to 8,000 nits of peak brightness, plus a 6.54-inch cover display. A magnetic flattening hinge keeps the crease as small as 15 microns. It runs on the Kirin 9050 Pro chip with 12GB of RAM and up to 2TB of storage. Photography is led by a 50MP main camera with a variable f/1.49-f/4.0 aperture and OIS, joined by a 40MP ultrawide and a 50MP 3.5x periscope telephoto with OIS, plus a third-generation Red Maple multispectral sensor, with up to 100x digital zoom. Two 8MP front cameras cover the inner and cover screens. The 6,200mAh battery supports 66W wired and 50W wireless charging. It ships with HarmonyOS 7 in a body that folds to 8.95mm and unfolds to 4.2mm, rated IP59, with a side-mounted fingerprint reader, eSIM, NFC and two-way BeiDou satellite messaging in China. An optional Ruiying Z10 clip-on camera adds a 50MP 1-inch sensor. The China price starts at CNY 12,999 for 12GB/256GB; no Pakistan launch or price has been announced, so the listed price is an expected price converted from the China launch price.

Gaming review

The Kirin 9050 Pro with 12GB of RAM and a 120Hz foldable display handles demanding 3D games at high settings, and the 6,200mAh battery keeps long sessions going without a recharge.

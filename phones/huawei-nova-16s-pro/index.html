Overview

The Huawei nova 16s Pro launched in Malaysia on 19 November 2026 as the photography flagship of the nova line. Its 6.84-inch LTPO OLED display adapts from 1Hz to 120Hz with a 6,000-nit peak, and a 7,000mAh battery with 100W SuperCharge Turbo sits in a slim 7.1mm, 218g body with IP65 protection and Kunlun Glass. The camera system is the reason to buy it: a 200MP RYYB main sensor with OIS, a 50MP RYYB periscope telephoto with 3.7x optical zoom and up to 100x digital zoom, a 50MP RYYB ultrawide, and True-to-Colour multispectral sensors on both the front and back for accurate skin tones. The Kirin 9010S chipset with 12GB of RAM delivers near-flagship speed, and a 50MP front camera with its own multispectral sensor targets selfie photography. On the software side it runs EMUI 16, without Google Mobile Services, as with other recent Huawei phones. It is aimed at buyers who want the most capable cameras in a mid-range Huawei phone, accepting the missing Google apps in return. In Malaysia it launched at RM 3,299 (12GB/512GB), with a RM 2,899 launch price after rebate; the expected Pakistan price is Rs 221,999, converted from the Malaysia launch price. Colours at the Malaysia launch: Breathing Crystal and Starry Black.

Gaming review

The Kirin 9010S with a 1-120Hz LTPO OLED runs demanding titles smoothly, though this is a camera-first phone — there is no active cooling or dedicated gaming hardware.

Our Verdict

The Huawei nova 16s Pro is a flagship-grade phone from Huawei, and its strongest suit is clear: smooth 120Hz LTPO OLED (FHD+ 2856x1320, 1-120Hz adaptive, HDR Vivid, 6000 nits peak) display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the HiSilicon Kirin 9010S chipset and a 7,000mAh battery with 100W charging. It runs EMUI 16 (Android-based) out of the box. It is one of the more rounded options in its bracket — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no headphone jack — wireless or usb-c audio only.

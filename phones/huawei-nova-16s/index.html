Overview

The Huawei nova 16s launched in Malaysia on 19 November 2026 as a camera-first mid-range phone. Its 6.68-inch OLED display runs at 120Hz with a 5,500-nit peak, and the phone is built around a 7,000mAh battery with 100W SuperCharge Turbo in a slim 7.3mm, 199g body. Photography is the headline: a 50MP main camera pairs with a 50MP RYYB periscope telephoto offering 3.3x optical zoom and up to 100x digital zoom, plus a True-to-Colour multispectral sensor for accurate skin tones, while a 50MP front camera handles selfies. The Kirin 8020 chipset with 12GB of RAM keeps everyday use and gaming smooth, and the phone carries IP65 protection, NFC, an infrared blaster and stereo speakers. On the software side it runs EMUI 16, without Google Mobile Services, as with other recent Huawei phones. It is aimed at buyers who want flagship-style zoom photography and huge battery life in a mid-range body. In Malaysia it launched at RM 2,599 (12GB/256GB), with a RM 1,999 launch price after rebate; the expected Pakistan price is Rs 174,999, converted from the Malaysia launch price. Colours: Breathing Crystal, Starry Black, Sky White.

Gaming review

The Kirin 8020 with a 120Hz OLED handles popular titles at sensible settings, but this is a camera-first phone, not a gaming phone — there is no active cooling or gaming trigger hardware.

Our Verdict

For Huawei, the Huawei nova 16s is a flagship-grade phone built around smooth 120Hz OLED (FHD+ 2800x1280, 120Hz, HDR Vivid, 5500 nits peak) display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the HiSilicon Kirin 8020 chipset and a 7,000mAh battery with 100W charging. It runs EMUI 16 (Android-based) out of the box. It makes a strong case for itself in this price band — with its main drawback understood upfront: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

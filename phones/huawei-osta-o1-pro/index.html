Overview

The Huawei OSTA O1 Pro launched in China on 20 November 2026 as the first phone from Huawei's new OSTA sub-brand, and it stands out immediately for its shape: a wide 6.39-inch LTPS OLED display with a 16:9.5 aspect ratio, 1.5K resolution (2232x1320), up to 120Hz and a 6,500-nit peak, with 1.05mm bezels on all four sides and Kunlun Glass. At 6.68mm thick and about 200g, it is slimmer than most phones with a battery this big. Photography is built around a 200MP RYYB main sensor (1/1.28-inch, OIS) and a 50MP RYYB periscope telephoto with 3.7x optical zoom (OIS), joined by a Red Maple multispectral colour sensor — there is no ultrawide camera, unlike the closely related Huawei Pura X View. Power comes from the Kirin 9030S chipset with 12GB of RAM, and the 7,000mAh battery charges at 66W wired plus 50W wireless, though chargers are sold separately. On the software side it is the first phone to ship with HarmonyOS 7 AI Pioneer Edition, with on-device AI features and no Google Play Services. It is aimed at buyers who want a genuinely different, wide-screen phone with flagship cameras and a huge battery. It is China-only for now, with sales from 20 November 2026 on Vmall and JD; the expected Pakistan price is Rs 234,999, converted from the China launch price of CNY 5,699.

Gaming review

The Kirin 9030S with a 120Hz 1.5K OLED and a 7,000mAh battery handles demanding titles well, and the wide 16:9.5 screen suits games and video; this is a camera-and-AI-first phone rather than a dedicated gaming device.

Our Verdict

The Huawei OSTA O1 Pro is one of Huawei's premium offerings, led by fast 120Hz LTPS OLED (1.5K 2232x1320, 120Hz, 6500 nits peak, Kunlun Glass) screen, a clear step above 60Hz panels. Headline hardware: the HiSilicon Kirin 9030S chipset and a 7,000mAh battery with 66W charging. Software is HarmonyOS 7 (AI Pioneer Edition) as listed by Huawei. It is one of the more rounded options in its bracket — look elsewhere if this matters to you: wired audio needs a usb-c adapter or dongle.

Overview

The Infinix Smart 10 is Infinix's budget phone, released in 2025. Its 6.67-inch IPS LCD display runs at 120Hz, so scrolling, animations and supported games feel noticeably smoother than on a standard 60Hz panel.

Power comes from the Unisoc T7250, which drives the phone's everyday performance, working alongside the RAM and storage on offer. The rear camera setup is 8MP main. Battery capacity is 5,000mAh, which is a full-day size for most users, and 15W charging means top-ups are reasonably brisk when you are in a hurry.

On the software side it runs Android 15 (Go edition). It is aimed at buyers who want a dependable everyday phone without overspending. In Pakistan it is listed at Rs 22,599 (market price, checked 2026-09-30) — confirm the final price, variant and warranty with the seller before paying.

Our Verdict

It packs the Unisoc T7250 chipset and a 5,000mAh battery with 15W charging. On the software side it runs Android 15 (Go edition). Is the Infinix Smart 10 PTA approved?

The Infinix Smart 10 is largely sold through market and import channels in Pakistan, so PTA status depends on the individual unit. Before paying, verify the IMEI via DIRBS (send the IMEI to 8484) and factor in the PTA tax if the unit is not already approved.

How is the Infinix Smart 10 battery life?

The Infinix Smart 10 carries a 5,000mAh battery with 15W fast charging. That is a large cell for this class, and most users should comfortably get a full day — heavier users with long gaming or video sessions may still charge overnight.

How good is the Infinix Smart 10 camera?

The rear setup is 8MP main, and the front camera is 8MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Unisoc T7250.

How long will the Infinix Smart 10 get software updates?

Is the Infinix Smart 10 good for gaming?

The Unisoc T7250 earns a "Weak" rating in our gaming assessment (score 1/5). Popular titles like PUBG Mobile and Call of Duty: Mobile are playable at settings that match the chipset's class; sustained heavy gaming will warm the phone up, as with most phones in this range.

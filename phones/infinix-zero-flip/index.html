Overview

Infinix's first flip phone presents a pricing puzzle in the record: WhatMobile.pk carries a market figure of Rs 234,999, while PriceOye lists a market price of Rs 119,500 — a difference large enough that checking which listing reflects real, available stock should be your first step before anything else.

The Zero Flip opens to a 6.9-inch foldable LTPO AMOLED screen at 120Hz with 1,400 nits peak brightness and 1080x2640 resolution. Inside is MediaTek's Dimensity 8020, a 6nm chip, with 8GB of RAM and a roomy 512GB of storage; the record ties gaming performance directly to that processor without offering a settings-level verdict, so expect mid-range silicon rather than flagship pace. Camera hardware is generous for a flip: a 50MP wide camera with OIS and a 50MP ultrawide at the back, and an unusually high-resolution 50MP front camera. There is no telephoto, so zoom means cropping. The 4,720mAh battery charges at 70W, quick by foldable standards. Software is Android 14 with XOS 14.5, and support is set at two years of OS updates and three years of security updates — short next to Samsung or Google.

Value hunters curious about foldables get a lot of storage and megapixels here. Anyone planning to keep a phone for four or five years will find the update window hard to accept.

Our Verdict

The Infinix Zero Flip arrives as a flagship-grade phone whose headline strength is fast 120Hz AMOLED screen, a clear step above 60Hz panels. It packs the MediaTek Dimensity 8020 (6 nm) chipset and a 4,720mAh battery with 70W charging. It deserves a place on the shortlist in this range — bearing in mind its clearest weakness: the 3.5mm headphone jack is missing.

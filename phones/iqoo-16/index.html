Overview

iQOO's 16 is a China-market gaming flagship whose expected price for Pakistan is Rs 248,400, converted from its CNY 5,999 launch price for 12GB/256GB. It is not officially launched here, while an India launch has been confirmed for late 2026 or early 2027, which may eventually change how easily units reach this market.

The display is a 6.85-inch Samsung M16 LTPO AMOLED at 2K resolution with a 165Hz refresh rate, Dolby Vision and 5,000Hz touch sampling. Performance pairs the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 with iQOO's own Q4 gaming chip, an 8,500mm² vapour chamber and direct-drive bypass power, earning an exceptional gaming rating and a place among the best sustained-performance gaming phones of 2026 in the record's judgement. Cameras are no afterthought: a 50MP main camera with OIS, a 50MP periscope telephoto with 3x zoom and OIS, and a 50MP ultrawide with autofocus, plus a 32MP selfie camera. The 8,400mAh battery takes 100W wired and 40W wireless charging. It runs Android 17 with OriginOS 7, bloatware is rated medium, and iQOO has not published an update policy.

Competitive gamers who value sustained frame rates above all else are the target. Buyers who need published update commitments or official Pakistan availability should hold off.

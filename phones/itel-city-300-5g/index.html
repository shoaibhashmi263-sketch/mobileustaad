Overview

The itel City 300 5G launched in India on October 14, 2026 through offline retail as a budget 5G option at Rs 14,999 (4GB RAM / 64GB storage). It pairs the 6nm Unisoc T8200 5G chipset with a 6.67-inch 120Hz HD+ LCD, a 13MP autofocus rear camera with 2K video recording, an 8MP front camera, a 5,000mAh battery with 10W wired charging, side-mounted fingerprint, a 3.5mm headphone jack, and Android 16 (Go Edition). No Pakistan launch has been announced, so no official Pakistan price exists.

Gaming review

The Unisoc T8200 and a 120Hz screen handle casual titles and free-to-play games like Free Fire at modest settings, but the HD+ display, 10W charging and entry-level GPU mean it is not a pick for heavy gamers. (Editorial estimate, not a measured benchmark.)

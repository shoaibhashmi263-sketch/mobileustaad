Overview

The Itel Zeno 20 Max launched in India on 8 January 2026 as an entry-level phone that puts durability and battery life first. Its 6.6-inch HD+ IPS display runs at 90Hz with up to 1,500 nits peak brightness and a Dynamic Bar around the front camera, and the body is rated IP54 with MIL-STD-810H shock resistance. It runs on the Unisoc T7100 chipset with 3GB or 4GB of RAM and 64GB of storage, with a single 13MP rear camera and an 8MP front camera. The 5,000mAh battery charges at 15W over USB-C, and a side-mounted fingerprint sensor, 3.5mm headphone jack and dedicated memory card slot cover the basics. It ships with itel OS 14 based on Android 14 (Go edition) and is a 4G-only phone. It has not been officially launched in Pakistan; the expected price is Rs 23,999 for the 3GB/64GB variant.

Gaming review

The Unisoc T7100 with a 90Hz HD+ display is built for light, casual games only; heavy titles will struggle, though the 5,000mAh battery is adequate for the class.

Overview

The Motorola Edge 70 Neo launched in India on 24 November 2026 as a mid-range all-rounder with a 6.8-inch 1.5K Extreme AMOLED display running at 144Hz with up to 5,200 nits of peak brightness and Gorilla Glass 7i protection. It runs on the Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chip with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage, expandable by microSD up to 1TB. Photography is led by a 50MP Sony LYTIA 710 main camera with optical stabilisation and 4K video, joined by a 2MP macro camera and a dedicated light sensor, while a 32MP front camera handles selfies in 4K. The 7,000mAh battery supports 45W TurboPower wired charging, 7.5W reverse charging and bypass charging. It runs Hello UI on Android 16 with 3 years of OS updates and 5 years of security patches promised, in a 204g body rated IP64 with MIL-STD-810H durability and stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos. The India price is Rs 40,999 (Rs 34,999 effective with launch offers); no Pakistan launch or price has been announced, so the listed price is an expected price converted from the India launch price.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 with 8GB of RAM and a 144Hz display runs PUBG fine on medium settings. Good for casual gaming on a budget.

Our Verdict

With the Motorola Edge 70 Neo, Motorola is aiming at buyers who want fast 144Hz Extreme AMOLED (2772x1272, 144Hz, HDR10+, 5200 nits peak, Gorilla Glass 7i) screen, a clear step above 60Hz panels without paying flagship money. Headline hardware: the Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 (4nm) chipset and a 7,000mAh battery with 45W charging. It runs Android 16 out of the box, with Motorola promising 3 years of Android updates. It deserves a place on the shortlist in this range — bearing in mind its clearest weakness: wired audio needs a usb-c adapter or dongle.

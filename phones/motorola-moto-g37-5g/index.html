Overview

Official availability is a central part of the Moto g37 5G story in Pakistan, where it arrived in October 2026 through distributor CoreTech as a PTA-approved model. The official price is Rs 59,999, while PriceOye shows a lower market price of Rs 57,495, and only one local configuration is offered: 4GB of RAM with 128GB of storage, expandable by microSD up to 1TB.

Motorola fits a 6.67-inch IPS LCD with a 120Hz refresh rate, which favours smooth scrolling and gaming motion over the contrast of an OLED panel. The Dimensity 6300 earns a Good gaming rating, running PUBG Mobile smoothly at medium settings and keeping casual titles fluid, although the record cautions against expecting high-refresh competitive play. Android 16 with Hello UX keeps bloatware low, and Motorola promises an upgrade to Android 17 together with three years of security patches. A side-mounted fingerprint reader, headphone jack, FM radio and Bluetooth 5.4 are present, while NFC depends on the market.

A single 50MP rear camera with an f/1.8 aperture and PDAF does all the photographic work, so there is no ultrawide or zoom flexibility, and the front camera is 8MP. The 5,200mAh battery supports 20W charging and can also deliver 6W reverse wired charging to another device. Consider it for official 5G with clean software and expandable storage; skip it if 4GB of RAM, slow charging or a lone rear camera feel too restrictive.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 6300 runs PUBG Mobile smoothly on medium settings; the 120Hz LCD keeps casual games fluid. Don't expect high-refresh competitive gaming at this price.

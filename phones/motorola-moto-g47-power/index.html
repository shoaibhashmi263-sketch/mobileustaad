Overview

The Motorola Moto G47 Power launched in Europe and the UK on 27 October 2026 as the big-battery member of the Moto G47 family, trading the standard G47's 5,200mAh cell for a 7,000mAh battery that Motorola rates at up to 78 hours of use. Its 6.67-inch LCD runs at 120Hz with 1,050 nits of high-brightness-mode brightness and Gorilla Glass 7i protection, and the MediaTek Dimensity 6300 chipset is paired with a single 4GB/128GB configuration plus microSD expansion to 1TB. Photography comes from a 108MP main camera with an f/1.7 aperture and 3x lossless zoom, joined by a 2MP macro camera, while an 8MP front camera handles selfies. The 7,000mAh battery (6,790mAh rated) charges at 30W and can reverse-charge other devices at 6W. The phone keeps the 3.5mm headphone jack, stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos, a side-mounted fingerprint sensor, NFC and IP64 splash resistance with MIL-STD-810H certification. On the software side it runs Android 16 with Motorola's Hello UI. It is aimed at buyers who value battery life and a headphone jack over RAM and screen resolution — the 4GB of RAM and HD+ display are the clear trade-offs for the huge battery. No Pakistan price or launch has been announced yet — the sole 4/128GB variant costs EUR 339 in Europe and GBP 299.99 in the UK. Colours: Pantone Capri, Pantone Nautical Blue and Pantone Impenetrable.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 6300 with 4GB of RAM and an HD+ 120Hz display handles casual games, but this is a battery-first budget phone — demanding titles and heavy multitasking will strain the modest memory.

Overview

Motorola's Signature 27 arrived globally in November 2026 with no official Pakistan launch, leaving grey-market imports as the route in, at a market price of Rs 359,999 listed by MobileInto for the single 12GB/512GB configuration. Its 6.78-inch Extreme AMOLED Duo tandem OLED display runs at 1.5K resolution and 120Hz, with a claimed 12,000 nits of peak brightness. The body combines Gorilla Glass Ceramic 3 with an aluminium frame, IP68 and IP69 ratings and MIL-STD-810H certification. A Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 processor earns an excellent gaming rating, described in the record as the most powerful chip yet and able to run any game with ease. The camera system mixes a 50MP main camera on a 1/1.28-inch Sony LYTIA 910 sensor with stabilisation, a 200MP 3x periscope telephoto that extends to 100x digital zoom, and a 50MP ultrawide, while the front camera is also 50MP. Bang & Olufsen-tuned audio and a dedicated EVS action sensor are included. Battery capacity is 7,000mAh from a silicon-carbon cell, with 90W wired and 50W wireless charging. Software is a strong point in this record: Android 17 with Hello UI, low bloatware, and a Motorola promise of seven years of OS and security updates. USB-C 3.1 Gen 2, NFC, Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6.0 are all included, without a headphone jack. It is built for buyers who want flagship cameras, durability ratings and long updates outside the usual brands; the grey-market purchase route is the main caution.

Overview

TechPrice shows the Nokia 2.1 at a market price of Rs 11,233, but context matters: the record says this 2018 model is discontinued with no current Pakistan price found. Its 5.5-inch IPS LCD runs at 720x1280, and the software is Android 8.1 Oreo in Go edition form. By current standards every part of that pairing signals a phone for the gentlest workloads only.

Inside is a Qualcomm Snapdragon 425 built on 28nm, with 1GB of RAM and 8GB of storage. Storage that small fills quickly, and gaming is rated weak, restricted to light casual games while PUBG Mobile or Free Fire will lag badly. Connections include microUSB 2.0, Bluetooth 4.2 and a headphone jack; there is no fingerprint reader and no NFC.

An 8MP autofocus rear camera and a 5MP front camera provide the imaging, with no secondary lenses mentioned. The bright spot is a 4,000mAh battery with 10W charging, generous capacity for such undemanding internals. As a cheap backup for calls and texts it can still serve if found in working order, but buyers needing app space, updates or speed should leave it behind.

Our Verdict

The Nokia 2.1 is a budget phone from Nokia, and its strongest suit is clear: roomy 5.5-inch IPS LCD panel. Headline hardware: the Qualcomm Snapdragon 425 (28 nm) chipset and a 4,000mAh battery with 10W charging. As a package it holds together well for the money — bearing in mind its clearest weakness: 10w charging is behind the curve now.

Is the Nokia 2.1 PTA approved?

How is the Nokia 2.1 battery life?

The Nokia 2.1 carries a 4,000mAh battery with 10W fast charging. It should see a typical user through the working day — heavy gaming will drain it faster.

How good is the Nokia 2.1 camera?

It pairs 8MP (AF) on the rear with a 5MP selfie camera. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class, daylight shooting is the strong suit, and low-light output leans on the Qualcomm Snapdragon 425 (28 nm)'s processing.

How long will the Nokia 2.1 get software updates?

Nokia has not published an update promise for this model.

Is the Nokia 2.1 good for gaming?

The Qualcomm Snapdragon 425 (28 nm) earns a "Weak" rating in our gaming assessment (score 1/5). Free Fire, PUBG Mobile and Call of Duty: Mobile are all playable at settings matched to the hardware — back-to-back gaming sessions will make it warm to hold.

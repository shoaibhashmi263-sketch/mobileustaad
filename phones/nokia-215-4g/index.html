Overview

The Nokia 215 4G (2024) is the camera-free member of HMD's 2024 feature-phone trio, with the largest display of the three at 2.8 inches. It shares the Unisoc T107 platform, Series 30+ OS, 1450mAh removable battery and USB-C charging with its siblings. With no camera at all, it is the purest digital-detox option of the lineup.

Overview

The Nokia 215 4G is Nokia's budget phone, released in 2024. Its 2.8-inch display is sized for comfortable everyday use — large enough for video and reading without making the phone awkward to handle.

Power comes from the Unisoc T107, which drives the phone's everyday performance, working alongside the RAM and storage on offer. The rear camera setup is None (rear torch only). Battery capacity is 1,450mAh.

It is aimed at buyers who want a dependable everyday phone without overspending. In Pakistan it is listed at Rs 5,999 (market price, checked 2026-10-06) — confirm the final price, variant and warranty with the seller before paying.

FAQs

What is the Nokia 215 4G price in Pakistan?

The Nokia 215 4G is available in Pakistan at Rs 5,999 (market price, source: HamariWeb (hamariweb.com), checked 2026-10-06). Prices can differ slightly between cities and shops, so confirm before you buy.

Is the Nokia 215 4G PTA approved?

The Nokia 215 4G is largely sold through market and import channels in Pakistan, so PTA status depends on the individual unit. Before paying, verify the IMEI via DIRBS (send the IMEI to 8484) and factor in the PTA tax if the unit is not already approved.

How is the Nokia 215 4G battery life?

The Nokia 215 4G carries a 1,450mAh battery. Most users should get through a typical day of calls, social media and video; heavy gaming will drain it faster.

How good is the Nokia 215 4G camera?

The rear setup is None (rear torch only), and the front camera is —MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Unisoc T107.

How long will the Nokia 215 4G get software updates?

Nokia has not published an update promise for this model.

Is the Nokia 215 4G good for gaming?

The Unisoc T107 earns a "Weak" rating in our gaming assessment (score 1/5). Popular titles like PUBG Mobile and Call of Duty: Mobile are playable at settings that match the chipset's class; sustained heavy gaming will warm the phone up, as with most phones in this range.

Overview

The Nokia 2360 Flip is HMD's new clamshell feature phone, launched in the Philippines on 2 November 2026 for seniors and school children who want a phone that behaves like a phone. It pairs a 2.8-inch QVGA main display with a 1.77-inch outer screen for checking callers without opening it, plus a physical keypad, FM radio, a 3.5mm jack and USB Type-C. Its headline features are safety ones: a dedicated orange SOS button that calls a preset contact and texts up to five more, and Family Assist, which lets a relative manage contacts, volume, reminders and SOS settings remotely by SMS — no data plan needed. Parental controls behind a Family PIN round out the package, and the removable 1450mAh battery is rated for up to 27.5 hours of talk time or 15 days of standby. With just 4MB of RAM and storage (microSD to 32GB), a QVGA camera and 2G-only connectivity, the 2360 Flip has no apps, no 4G and no social media — deliberately. It is a simplicity-and-safety phone, not a smartphone, and it has no Pakistan launch or price yet.

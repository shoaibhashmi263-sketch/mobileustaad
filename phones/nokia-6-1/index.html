Overview

HamariWeb lists a market price of Rs 28,799 for the Nokia 6.1, while the record also states the phone is discontinued with no current Pakistan price found, so that number is best understood as a leftover listing rather than a live offer. Originally launched in early 2018, this is now firmly a second-hand or collector-style purchase rather than a sensible new-phone buy.

The 5.5-inch IPS LCD delivers 1080x1920 Full HD resolution behind Gorilla Glass 3, a respectable sharpness level for video and reading despite the smaller, older-format screen. Underneath sits a Snapdragon 630 with 3GB of RAM and 32GB of storage, described in the gaming notes as an ultra-budget chip for ultra-basic needs, capable of Snake-like casual games rather than modern 3D titles. It runs Android 8.1 Oreo under Android One and still offers useful touches including NFC, a headphone jack, USB Type-C, Bluetooth 5.0 and a rear fingerprint reader.

A single 16MP rear camera with PDAF handles all photography, with no depth, ultrawide or zoom support, and an 8MP camera covers selfies and video calls. The 3,000mAh battery is modest, although 18W charging helps it recover faster than slower-charging contemporaries. It could suit a minimal user wanting a compact backup for calls and messages, but anyone expecting current app performance, storage space or gaming ability should pass.

Our Verdict

Positioned as a budget phone, the Nokia 6.1 puts roomy 5.5-inch IPS LCD panel at the top of its pitch. Headline hardware: the Qualcomm Snapdragon 630 (14 nm) chipset and a 3,000mAh battery with 18W charging. As a package it holds together well for the money — with its main drawback understood upfront: only 18w charging — full top-ups take a while.

Is the Nokia 6.1 PTA approved?

Because the Nokia 6.1 is mostly sold via import channels here, its PTA status varies from unit to unit. So first check the IMEI in the DIRBS system (text it to 8484) and remember an unapproved unit will need its PTA tax paid separately.

How is the Nokia 6.1 battery life?

The Nokia 6.1 carries a 3,000mAh battery with 18W fast charging. Day-to-day use should be fine on a single charge, though a heavy camera day will pull it down sooner.

How good is the Nokia 6.1 camera?

Cameras are 16MP wide (f/2.0, PDAF) at the back and 8MP at the front. On specification alone it competes well in this price class, daylight shooting is the strong suit, and low-light output leans on the Qualcomm Snapdragon 630 (14 nm)'s processing.

How long will the Nokia 6.1 get software updates?

For the Nokia 6.1, Nokia has not published an update commitment.

Is the Nokia 6.1 good for gaming?

The Qualcomm Snapdragon 630 (14 nm) scores a "Average" rating in our gaming assessment (score 2/5). Free Fire, PUBG Mobile and Call of Duty: Mobile are all playable at settings matched to the hardware, heat is noticeable after extended play, though no worse than its rivals.

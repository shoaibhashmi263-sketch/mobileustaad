Overview

Nokia revived a familiar name in November 2020 with the 6300 4G, a KaiOS feature phone that adds 4G connectivity to a classic candybar shape. PriceOye lists it at a market price of Rs 9,299, but the record notes the model is discontinued in Pakistan, so that is a last-updated figure with no current retail availability behind it.

The 2.4-inch TFT display has a 240x320 resolution, enough for menus, messages and short text rather than video. A Qualcomm Snapdragon 210 chip works with 0.5GB of RAM and 4GB of storage, and the gaming verdict in the record is weak, with only light casual games running smoothly and PUBG Mobile expected to lag badly. Photography rests on a single VGA rear camera with an LED flash, which is suitable for little more than quick reference shots, and there is no front camera. The 1,500mAh battery is small even by feature-phone standards, charging details are not published, and connections run through microUSB with Bluetooth 4.0 and a headphone jack.

Pick it if you want physical keys, 4G support and a deliberately simple phone for calls and texts. Leave it aside if you rely on a proper camera, long stretches away from a charger or a wide choice of smartphone apps.

Our Verdict

The Nokia 6300 4G arrives as a budget phone whose headline strength is roomy 2.4-inch TFT panel. It packs the Qualcomm Snapdragon 210 chipset and a 1,500mAh battery. For most buyers in this bracket it is easy to recommend — with one caveat to weigh first: smaller 1,500mah battery than most rivals.

How is the Nokia 6300 4G battery life?

The Nokia 6300 4G carries a 1,500mAh battery. For everyday use — calls, social apps and some video — a full day is realistic — with heavy gaming eating into it noticeably faster.

How good is the Nokia 6300 4G camera?

Cameras are VGA with LED flash at the back and —MP at the front. On specification alone it competes well in this price class, good light flatters it most, with low-light quality tied to the Qualcomm Snapdragon 210's tuning.

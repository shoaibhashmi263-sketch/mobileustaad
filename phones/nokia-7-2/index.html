Overview

The 48MP upgrade to the 7 series, announced at IFA 2019. It brings a Snapdragon 660, 6.3-inch FHD+ HDR10 PureDisplay, 48MP + 8MP ultrawide + 5MP depth ZEISS triple cameras with a dedicated night mode, and a 20MP front camera. The Verge called it Nokia's 'most confident creation yet' while noting the slow 10W charging.

Our Verdict

The Nokia 7.2 is a budget phone that leads with large 6.3-inch IPS LCD display. It packs the Qualcomm Snapdragon 660 (14 nm) chipset and a 3,500mAh battery with 10W charging. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: charging is slow at 10w.

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Large 6.3-inch IPS LCD display
  • NFC on board for contactless payments
  • 3.5mm headphone jack is still included

Cons

  • Charging is slow at 10W
  • Battery is on the smaller side at 3,500mAh
  • Entry-level chipset — fine for daily use, not for heavy gaming
  • Nokia has not promised a software update period for this model

FAQs

What is the Nokia 7.2 price in Pakistan?

The Nokia 7.2 is available in Pakistan at Rs 39,999 (market price, source: PhoneBolee (phonebolee.com), checked 2026-10-05). Prices can differ slightly between cities and shops, so confirm before you buy.

Is the Nokia 7.2 PTA approved?

The Nokia 7.2 is largely sold through market and import channels in Pakistan, so PTA status depends on the individual unit. Before paying, verify the IMEI via DIRBS (send the IMEI to 8484) and factor in the PTA tax if the unit is not already approved.

How is the Nokia 7.2 battery life?

The Nokia 7.2 carries a 3,500mAh battery with 10W fast charging. Most users should get through a typical day of calls, social media and video; heavy gaming will drain it faster.

How good is the Nokia 7.2 camera?

The rear setup is 48MP wide (f/1.8, PDAF) + 8MP ultrawide + 5MP depth, and the front camera is 20MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Qualcomm Snapdragon 660 (14 nm).

How long will the Nokia 7.2 get software updates?

Nokia has not published an update promise for this model.

Is the Nokia 7.2 good for gaming?

The Qualcomm Snapdragon 660 (14 nm) earns a "Average" rating in our gaming assessment (score 2/5). Popular titles like PUBG Mobile and Call of Duty: Mobile are playable at settings that match the chipset's class; sustained heavy gaming will warm the phone up, as with most phones in this range.

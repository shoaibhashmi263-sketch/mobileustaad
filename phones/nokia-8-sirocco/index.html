Overview

The Nokia 8 Sirocco is Nokia's premium mid-range phone, released in 2018. Its 5.5-inch OLED display delivers deep blacks and strong contrast, which makes video and social feeds look richer than on a plain LCD.

Power comes from the Qualcomm Snapdragon 835 (10 nm), which drives the phone's everyday performance, working alongside the RAM and storage on offer. The rear camera setup is 12MP wide (f/1.8, dual pixel PDAF) + 13MP telephoto (2x optical zoom). Battery capacity is 3,260mAh, and 18W charging means top-ups are reasonably brisk when you are in a hurry.

It is aimed at buyers who want premium features without paying full flagship money. In Pakistan it is listed at Rs 94,000 (market price, checked 2026-10-06) — confirm the final price, variant and warranty with the seller before paying.

Our Verdict

The Nokia 8 Sirocco is a premium mid-range phone that leads with large 5.5-inch OLED display. It packs the Qualcomm Snapdragon 835 (10 nm) chipset and a 3,260mAh battery with 18W charging. Buy it if the strengths above match how you use a phone; look elsewhere if this matters to you: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

The Nokia 8 Sirocco carries a 3,260mAh battery with 18W fast charging. Most users should get through a typical day of calls, social media and video; heavy gaming will drain it faster.

How good is the Nokia 8 Sirocco camera?

The rear setup is 12MP wide (f/1.8, dual pixel PDAF) + 13MP telephoto (2x optical zoom), and the front camera is 5MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Qualcomm Snapdragon 835 (10 nm).

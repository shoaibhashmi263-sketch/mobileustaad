Overview

Curved glass and a compact footprint define this 2018 Nokia, at a market price of Rs 94,000 via HamariWeb even though it is discontinued, with no current Pakistan price found. Up front sits a 5.5-inch curved pOLED panel at sharp 1440x2560 QHD resolution behind Gorilla Glass 5. There is no headphone jack, though NFC, Bluetooth 5.0 and USB Type-C 3.0 are present, and unlocking is handled by a rear-mounted fingerprint sensor.

Inside, a Snapdragon 835 on a 10nm process works with 6GB of RAM and 128GB of storage. The record credits that former flagship chip with solid PUBG Mobile play and competence in mid-tier titles, while warning it is not suited to modern games at maximum settings. Its rear pair combines a 12MP wide camera with dual pixel PDAF and f/1.8 aperture alongside a 13MP telephoto offering 2x optical zoom, a genuinely useful extra most phones here lack. Selfies, by contrast, rely on only a 5MP front camera. Power comes from a small 3,260mAh cell with 18W charging, so heavy users will be reaching for a charger sooner than owners of larger-battery phones.

Software started on Android 8.0 Oreo under Android One, with no published update promise listed, which matters at this age. Consider it only if the curved QHD screen, optical zoom and compact premium build appeal to you as a niche, older flagship. Most buyers should look elsewhere for a bigger battery, a stronger selfie camera, 5G, or any certainty about ongoing support and present-day availability.

Gaming review

Snapdragon 835 flagship — solid for PUBG Mobile and mid-tier titles; not for max-settings modern games.

Our Verdict

Positioned as a premium mid-range phone, the Nokia 8 Sirocco puts large 5.5-inch OLED display at the top of its pitch. Headline hardware: the Qualcomm Snapdragon 835 (10 nm) chipset and a 3,260mAh battery with 18W charging. Shortlist it if the spec mix suits you — with one caveat to weigh first: wired audio needs a usb-c adapter or dongle.

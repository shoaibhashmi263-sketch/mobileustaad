Overview

The refined successor to the Nokia 8, introduced at MWC 2018 and named after the classic Nokia 8800 Sirocco. It upgrades to a curved 5.5-inch pOLED QHD display, stainless-steel frame with glass back, 12MP + 13MP telephoto ZEISS cameras, Qi wireless charging and IP67 water resistance.

Gaming review

Snapdragon 835 flagship — solid for PUBG Mobile and mid-tier titles; not for max-settings modern games.

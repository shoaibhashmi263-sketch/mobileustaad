Overview

The Nokia C31 is Nokia's budget phone, released in 2022. Its 6.75-inch IPS LCD display is sized for comfortable everyday use — large enough for video and reading without making the phone awkward to handle.

Power comes from the Unisoc SC9863A (28 nm), which drives the phone's everyday performance, working alongside the RAM and storage on offer. The rear camera setup is 13MP (wide, AF) + 2MP (macro) + 2MP (depth). Battery capacity is 5,050mAh, which is a full-day size for most users, and 10W charging means top-ups are reasonably brisk when you are in a hurry.

It is aimed at buyers who want a dependable everyday phone without overspending. In Pakistan it is listed at Rs 41,999 (market price, checked 2026-10-25) — confirm the final price, variant and warranty with the seller before paying.

Our Verdict

The Nokia C31 is a budget phone from Nokia, and its strongest suit is clear: large 6.75-inch IPS LCD display. It packs the Unisoc SC9863A (28 nm) chipset and a 5,050mAh battery with 10W charging. Buy it if the strengths above match how you use a phone; look elsewhere if this matters to you: charging is slow at 10w.

FAQs

What is the Nokia C31 price in Pakistan?

The Nokia C31 is available in Pakistan at Rs 41,999 (market price, source: whatmobile.com.pk, checked 2026-10-25). Prices can differ slightly between cities and shops, so confirm before you buy.

Is the Nokia C31 PTA approved?

The Nokia C31 is largely sold through market and import channels in Pakistan, so PTA status depends on the individual unit. Before paying, verify the IMEI via DIRBS (send the IMEI to 8484) and factor in the PTA tax if the unit is not already approved.

How is the Nokia C31 battery life?

The Nokia C31 carries a 5,050mAh battery with 10W fast charging. That is a large cell for this class, and most users should comfortably get a full day — heavier users with long gaming or video sessions may still charge overnight.

How good is the Nokia C31 camera?

The rear setup is 13MP (wide, AF) + 2MP (macro) + 2MP (depth), and the front camera is 5MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Unisoc SC9863A (28 nm).

How long will the Nokia C31 get software updates?

Nokia has not published an update promise for this model.

Is the Nokia C31 good for gaming?

The Unisoc SC9863A (28 nm) earns a "Weak" rating in our gaming assessment (score 1/5). Popular titles like PUBG Mobile and Call of Duty: Mobile are playable at settings that match the chipset's class; sustained heavy gaming will warm the phone up, as with most phones in this range.

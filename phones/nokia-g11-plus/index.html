Overview

Nokia's G11 Plus, a July 2022 model, is listed by mobilemall.com.pk at a market price of Rs 47,999, drawn from the Nokia distributor list, while WhatMobile has shown it as coming soon with an expected figure of Rs 49,999. Either way, expectations should be set low: the 6.52-inch IPS LCD has a 720x1600 resolution, softened detail in exchange for a 90Hz refresh rate that keeps scrolling pleasant. Storage is just 64GB alongside 4GB of RAM, and there is a headphone jack but no NFC and no 5G.

The Unisoc T606 processor explains the record's Weak gaming label: only the lightest games run well, and anything 3D-heavy is likely to feel frustrating and laggy. This is hardware for calls, messaging and light browsing. Software starts at Android 12 and is upgradable to Android 13 per the record, with no published update years beyond that.

A 50MP wide rear camera is assisted by a 2MP depth sensor, so the second lens only supports portrait effects rather than adding a new viewpoint, and the front camera is 8MP. The 5,000mAh battery is generously sized, yet 10W charging means refills will take a long while. The G11 Plus fits buyers who need a simple, affordable Nokia for basics and can accept slow charging; anyone wanting storage space, gaming ability or 5G should spend elsewhere.

Our Verdict

Positioned as a mid-range phone, the Nokia G11 Plus puts large 6.52-inch IPS LCD display at the top of its pitch. It packs the Unisoc T606 (12 nm) chipset and a 5,000mAh battery with 10W charging. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — with one caveat to weigh first: 10w charging is behind the curve now.

The rear camera system is 50MP (wide) + 2MP (depth); selfies use a 8MP front camera. On specification alone it competes well in this price class, good light flatters it most, with low-light quality tied to the Unisoc T606 (12 nm)'s tuning.

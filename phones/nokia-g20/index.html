Overview

The Nokia G20 was the more capable sibling of the G10, with a 48MP quad camera including an ultrawide lens and the faster Helio G35 chip. It kept the 5050mAh battery and clean Android software with two OS upgrades promised. It was one of Nokia's best-selling budget phones in Pakistan.

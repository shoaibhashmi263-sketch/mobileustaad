Overview

The Nokia X30 5G was HMD's eco-flagship for 2022, built with a 100% recycled aluminium frame and a 65% recycled plastic back. Its 50MP PureView camera with optical image stabilisation and an IP67 rating were firsts for the brand at this tier. It shipped with Android 12 and a promise of three OS upgrades.

Overview

The Nothing Phone (3a) Pro is Nothing's premium mid-range phone, released in 2025. Its 6.77-inch AMOLED display runs at 120Hz, so scrolling, animations and supported games feel noticeably smoother than on a standard 60Hz panel.

Power comes from the Snapdragon 7s Gen 3, which keeps everyday apps quick and handles popular games smoothly at sensible settings. The rear camera setup is 50MP OIS + 50MP 3x periscope + 8MP ultrawide, and the high-resolution main sensor is the one you will lean on for daylight shots, which come out detailed. Battery capacity is 5,000mAh, which is a full-day size for most users, and 50W charging means top-ups are genuinely quick when you are in a hurry.

On the software side it runs Android 15, and Nothing promises 3 years of Android updates, so it stays current for longer than most rivals at this price. It is aimed at buyers who want premium features without paying full flagship money. Our Verdict

The Nothing Phone (3a) Pro is a premium mid-range phone from Nothing, and its strongest suit is clear: smooth 120Hz AMOLED display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chipset and a 5,000mAh battery with 50W charging. On the software side it runs Android 15, and Nothing promises 3 years of Android updates. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

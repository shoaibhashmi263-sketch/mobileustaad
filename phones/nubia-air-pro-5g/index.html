Overview

The nubia Air Pro 5G is ZTE's ultra-thin premium mid-ranger, just 5.99mm thick with a metal frame, and it reached Pakistan on 2 November 2026 alongside the Neo 3 5G. Its 6.77-inch AMOLED runs at 144Hz with a 1.5K resolution and a 4500-nit peak brightness, and the phone carries an IP69K rating despite its slim body. Cameras are a 108MP main sensor without optical stabilisation plus an auxiliary lens, with a 32MP punch-hole front camera. Power comes from the MediaTek Dimensity 7100 with 8GB of RAM and, in Pakistan, 256GB of storage. The 5000mAh battery charges at 44W wired with 10W reverse wired charging and bypass charging, and the fingerprint reader sits under the display. At 172g, it is one of the lightest phones in its class. On Android 16, the Air Pro 5G is for buyers who want a slim, light phone with a fast, bright screen and a high-resolution camera at Rs 109,999 — a design-led alternative to the chunkier gaming phones in nubia's own range.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 7100 with a 144Hz AMOLED, bypass charging and a 5000mAh battery is a strong mid-range gaming combination — smooth play in most titles at high settings, with only the most demanding games needing a step down.

Our Verdict

With the Nubia Air Pro 5G, Nubia is aiming at buyers who want smooth 144Hz AMOLED, 1.5K (1224x2720), 4500 nits peak, 3840Hz PWM display that feels fast in everyday use without paying flagship money. It packs the MediaTek Dimensity 7100 chipset and a 5,000mAh battery with 44W charging. On the software side it runs Android 16. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

How good is the Nubia Air Pro 5G camera?

The rear setup is 108MP main (no OIS) + auxiliary lens, and the front camera is 32MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the MediaTek Dimensity 7100.

Overview

The Nubia Neo 5 Max PadPhone 5G is a gaming phone built around a tablet-sized 7.5-inch 1.5K IPS LCD with a 120Hz refresh rate, launched in Pakistan on 31 October 2026 and on sale from 2 November at Rs 99,999. The MediaTek Dimensity 7100 pairs with 8GB of LPDDR5X RAM and 256GB of storage, while dedicated shoulder triggers with a 990Hz touch sampling rate, a 30,000mm² cooling area and bypass charging mark it out as a purpose-built gaming device rather than a standard mid-ranger with a gaming badge. The huge 7,100mAh battery with 45W wired charging and 10W reverse charging keeps the big screen running through long sessions, and the camera setup is a 50MP main sensor with optical stabilisation plus a 2MP depth sensor, with a 16MP front camera. Stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos, an IP64 splash-resistant build and a side-mounted fingerprint reader round out a spec sheet that is unusually generous for the price. On Android 16 with nubia's MyOS, Demi AI assistance and AI Game Space, the Neo 5 Max is aimed squarely at mobile gamers who want the largest screen available at this price — a genuine pocket tablet for PUBG and similar titles — and its official Pakistan price undercuts every other gaming phone with this screen size.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 7100 is a mid-range chip, but the 990Hz shoulder triggers, 30,000mm² cooling, bypass charging and 7,100mAh battery make sustained gaming genuinely strong — the 120Hz 7.5-inch screen shows more of the game than any rival at this price. Heavy titles will still need medium settings.

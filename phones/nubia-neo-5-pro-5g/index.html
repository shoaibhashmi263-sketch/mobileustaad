Overview

The Nubia Neo 5 Pro 5G is the smaller, pricier sibling of the Neo 5 Max, trading the giant tablet-sized screen for a 6.8-inch FHD+ AMOLED with a 120Hz refresh rate and 1,300 nits peak brightness, launched in Pakistan alongside the Max on 31 October 2026 at Rs 119,999. It runs the UNISOC T9100 with 12GB of physical RAM and 256GB of storage — the only variant shipped in Pakistan — and keeps the series' gaming identity with shoulder triggers, an IP64 splash-resistant build and stereo speakers. Its 6,000mAh battery charges at 80W with the charger included in the box, plus bypass charging for long plugged-in sessions, and the 50MP main camera with a 2MP depth sensor records 4K video at 30fps — a step up from the Max's 1080p. An in-display optical fingerprint reader replaces the Max's side-mounted key. On Android 16 with MyOS, the Pro suits gamers who want an OLED screen and a pocketable size rather than the Max's 7.5-inch slab, though the T9100 chip is the weaker of the two for demanding titles and the price premium buys the screen and faster charging, not more speed.

Gaming review

The UNISOC T9100 with Mali-G57 graphics is a modest gaming chip, so heavy titles need medium settings, but the 120Hz OLED, shoulder triggers, bypass charging and 80W top-ups make it a capable casual-to-mid gaming phone with a much better screen than most rivals at this price.

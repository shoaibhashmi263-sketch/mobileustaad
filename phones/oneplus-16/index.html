Overview

The OnePlus 16 is OnePlus's 2026 flagship: a flat 6.78-inch 165Hz BOE X4 AMOLED (185Hz in gaming mode) driven by the Fengchi Gaming Kernel with dedicated G3 (network), Lingxi (touch) and P4 (display) esports chips, the 2nm Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, a 200MP main camera with a 50MP 3x periscope telephoto, a 50MP autofocus selfie camera, and a 9,000mAh battery with 100W wired + 50W wireless charging and bypass charging.

Gaming review

Built for esports: Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 plus the Fengchi Gaming Kernel, dedicated G3 (network), Lingxi (touch) and P4 (display) gaming chips, a 185Hz gaming mode and one-click 'Gun God Mode', with a 9,000mAh battery, bypass charging and the Glacier Cooling System for long sessions — OnePlus's strongest gaming phone to date.

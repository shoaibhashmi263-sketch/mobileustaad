Overview

The OnePlus Ace 7 launched in China on 29 October 2026 as a gaming-first sub-flagship, headlined by a built-in active cooling fan, a 9,000mAh battery and a 185Hz gaming display. Its 6.78-inch 1.5K LTPO OLED panel runs at 1-165Hz in normal use and up to 185Hz in supported games, and the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 V Series chipset is backed by dedicated T3 touch, G3 network and P4 display gaming chips plus a vapour-chamber cooling system. Photography comes from a 200MP Samsung HP5 main camera with optical image stabilisation, joined by an 8MP ultrawide, while a 16MP front camera handles selfies. The 9,000mAh battery charges at 100W SuperVOOC, supports bypass charging so power goes straight to the phone during play, and can reverse-charge other devices at 27W. The phone is rated IP66, IP68, IP69 and IP69K, and carries a 3D ultrasonic in-display fingerprint sensor, Wi-Fi 7, NFC and an infrared blaster. On the software side it runs Android 17 with ColorOS 17 in China. It is aimed squarely at mobile gamers who want flagship speed and all-day battery without flagship pricing. No Pakistan price or launch has been announced yet — the China prices start at CNY 4,399 (12/256GB), rising to CNY 5,699 (16/512GB). Colours: Polar Night Black, Glossy White and a grey-to-gold gradient. The standard Ace 7 is tipped to arrive in India as the OnePlus 16R, but OnePlus has not confirmed that.

Gaming review

A gaming phone through and through: Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 V Series, a built-in cooling fan, 185Hz gaming display, dedicated touch/network/display gaming chips and bypass charging, all fed by a 9,000mAh battery.

Our Verdict

The OnePlus Ace 7 is one of OnePlus's premium offerings, led by fast 185Hz LTPO OLED, 1.5K (2772x1272, 1-165Hz, 185Hz in supported games, 2000 nits peak, Crystal Shield Glass) screen, a clear step above 60Hz panels. It packs the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 V Series (3nm) chipset and a 9,000mAh battery with 100W charging. Software is Android 17 (ColorOS 17) as listed by OnePlus. It is one of the more rounded options in its bracket — though buyers should weigh this first: the 3.5mm headphone jack is missing.

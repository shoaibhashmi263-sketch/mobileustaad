Overview

The Oppo A7 Pro Max launched in China on 4 August 2026 as a durability-first midrange phone, built around the Qualcomm Snapdragon 4 Gen 5 chipset with 8GB of RAM and up to 512GB of storage. Its 6.78-inch AMOLED display runs at 120Hz with 1272x2772 resolution, a billion colours and 1,800 nits of peak brightness under Crystal Guard glass. Photography is led by a 50MP main camera with optical image stabilisation, joined by a 2MP monochrome sensor, while the front camera is an unusually strong 50MP ultra-wide unit with autofocus for group selfies. The 10,000mAh battery charges at 80W SUPERVOOC with 27W reverse wired charging, and a bright Power Flash helps in low light. It runs Android 16 with ColorOS 16 in a rugged 224g body rated IP66, IP68, IP69 and IP69K with MIL-STD-810H shock resistance and a multi-colour breathing notification light on the back. The Pakistan launch is expected on 30 November 2026, with an expected price of Rs 94,999 for the 8GB/128GB variant.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 4 Gen 5 is a budget-tier 5G chip — smooth for casual games and daily play on the 120Hz AMOLED display, but demanding titles need medium-low settings. The 10,000mAh battery is the real gaming asset: very long sessions without a charger.

Overview

The Oppo A7 Pro launched in China on September 11, 2026 and reached Europe on November 12, 2026 as a durability-first mid-ranger. It pairs a 6.57-inch AMOLED display (120Hz, 1,400-nit peak) with the MediaTek Dimensity 6360 Max, 6GB or 8GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of expandable storage, all wrapped in an IP68/IP69K body with military-grade shock resistance and drop protection to 1.8m. The headline feature is endurance: an 8,000mAh battery with 45W wired charging on the global variant (the Europe variant swaps to a 7,400mAh cell with faster 80W charging). The rear camera is a 50MP main sensor with OIS plus a 2MP monochrome helper, and the global variant carries a 50MP ultrawide selfie camera, while the China variant has an 8MP front camera. The phone ships with Android 16 and ColorOS 16, with Oppo stating 5 years of OS updates and 6 years of security updates, plus a 5-year smoothness protection claim. It is built for buyers who want a tough, long-lasting phone rather than a camera flagship. There is no Pakistan launch or price yet; in Europe it lists at EUR 499.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 6360 Max is an entry-midrange chip that handles casual and mid-tier games well; demanding titles need lowered settings on the 120Hz AMOLED display. The huge 8,000mAh battery is the real gaming asset — long sessions without hunting for a charger.

Overview

The OPPO Find X10 E launched in China on September 24, 2026 as the entry point of the Find X10 family — it was not part of the October global/India launch. It runs the 3nm MediaTek Dimensity 9500s, a 6.59-inch 120Hz AMOLED (2760x1256, 1,800-nit peak, Dolby Vision) and a Hasselblad-tuned triple-50MP camera system: 50MP 1/1.4-inch f/1.6 OIS main, 50MP 3x periscope telephoto with OIS, and 50MP f/2.0 ultrawide, driven by the LUMO imaging engine. A 7,025mAh battery charges at 80W wired + 50W wireless, with a 32MP front camera, IP66+IP68+IP69 ratings, in-display ultrasonic fingerprint sensor and ColorOS 17 on Android 17. 156.98 x 73.93 x 7.99mm, 203g. Colors: Light Titanium, Ice Blue.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 9500s (~2.7M AnTuTu v11, sustained-throttle-friendly) with a 120Hz AMOLED and 7,025mAh battery is flagship-adjacent for gaming, though the X10 E is tuned for cameras first — no gaming triggers or active cooling. (Editorial estimate, not a measured benchmark.)

Price notes

China-only as of Oct 2026; global rollout possible (the Find X9s traveled globally earlier in 2026) but unconfirmed. Effectively an evolved Find X9s: adds 50W wireless charging, a larger 1/1.4-inch main sensor and a stabilized 3x telephoto.

Our Verdict

The OPPO Find X10 E is a phone that leads with smooth 120Hz AMOLED, 120Hz, 3840Hz PWM, Dolby Vision, 1800 nits peak display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the MediaTek Dimensity 9500s (3nm) chipset and a 7,025mAh battery with 80W charging. On the software side it runs Android 17. Buy it if the strengths above match how you use a phone; look elsewhere if this matters to you: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

Overview

The OPPO Find X10 Pro Max is the first phone in the world with three 200MP cameras, debuting globally in Mallorca on October 21, 2026. It runs the 2nm Dimensity 9600 Pro, a 6.78-inch 2K LTPO AMOLED (120Hz, 144Hz in games), and a Hasselblad triple-200MP system — 200MP 1/1.3-inch OIS main, 200MP ultrawide and 200MP 3x periscope macro — with the LUMO True Image Engine and a dedicated True Color camera. The Luna White finish takes cues from classic cameras with knurled detailing and Hasselblad's orange accent ring. A 7,150mAh battery (8,000mAh in China) charges at 80W + 50W wireless, with Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6.2, USB-C 3.2, IP66+IP68+IP69 and Android 17 with ColorOS 17. Colors: Titanium Grey, Luna White (global); Moon White, Warm Orange, Light Titanium (China).

Gaming review

The 2nm Dimensity 9600 Pro with a 144Hz gaming mode, 7,150mAh battery and 80W charging is top-tier silicon, but this is a camera flagship first — no active cooling or gaming triggers. (Editorial estimate, not a measured benchmark.)

Our Verdict

The Oppo Find X10 Pro Max is a phone from Oppo, and its strongest suit is clear: smooth 120Hz LTPO AMOLED, 2772x1272, 120Hz (144Hz gaming), 3600 nits peak, 3840Hz PWM dimming display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro (2nm) chipset and a 7,150mAh battery with 80W charging. On the software side it runs Android 17. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

Overview

Camera hardware dominates the Oppo Find X10 story. It has not been officially launched in Pakistan, and PriceOye lists a market price of Rs 129,999; the record notes that local listings are imported units, so PTA status and warranty need checking before buying. The phone itself uses a relatively compact 6.59-inch AMOLED screen with a 144Hz refresh rate and a borderless sculpted-glass design, plus an ultrasonic in-display fingerprint reader.

The rear system is remarkable on paper: a 200MP Hasselblad main camera with a large 1/1.4-inch sensor, f/1.6 aperture and OIS, a second 200MP periscope camera offering 2.8x zoom with OIS, and a 50MP ultrawide. A 50MP front camera handles selfies. MediaTek's 3nm Dimensity 9600M earns an Exceptional gaming rating, although the record notes the phone is tuned for cameras first, with no dedicated gaming triggers or active cooling.

A 7,150mAh battery supports 80W wired and 50W wireless charging, giving flagship-style flexibility. Software is Android 17 with ColorOS 17, but Oppo has not published OS or security update durations for this model, and Bluetooth version is likewise unpublished. Buy it if serious zoom and main-camera hardware are your top priorities and you accept the imported-unit route; skip it if official local availability and a stated update policy are non-negotiable.

Our Verdict

The Oppo Find X10 is a premium mid-range phone that leads with high-refresh 144Hz AMOLED, 144Hz, Borderless One-Piece Sculpted Glass design display for smoother everyday use. It packs the MediaTek Dimensity 9600M (3nm) chipset and a 7,150mAh battery with 80W charging. It runs Android 17 out of the box. It deserves a place on the shortlist in this range — bearing in mind its clearest weakness: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

Overview

The OPPO Find X10 debuted globally in Mallorca on October 21, 2026 (China launch September 22) as a camera-first flagship. It runs the 3nm Dimensity 9600M, a 6.59-inch 144Hz AMOLED in a Borderless One-Piece Sculpted Glass body, and a Hasselblad dual-200MP camera system (200MP 1/1.4-inch OIS main + 200MP 2.8x periscope OIS + 50MP ultrawide) driven by the LUMO True Image Engine. A 7,150mAh battery (8,000mAh in China) charges at 80W wired + 50W wireless, with a 50MP front camera, IP66+IP68+IP69 ratings and Android 17 with ColorOS 17. Colors: Glacier Blue, Glow Coral, Mist Grey.

Gaming review

The 3nm Dimensity 9600M with a 144Hz AMOLED and 7,150mAh battery (80W charging) is flagship-class for gaming, though the Find X10 is tuned for cameras first — no dedicated gaming triggers or active cooling. (Editorial estimate, not a measured benchmark.)

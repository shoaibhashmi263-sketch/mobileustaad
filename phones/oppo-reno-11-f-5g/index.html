Overview

With an official price of Rs 84,999 recorded from WhatMobile, and a market price of Rs 78,899 on PriceOye, this Oppo from February 2024 targets buyers who prioritise screen quality and selfies. The 6.7-inch AMOLED panel displays 1B colours at 1080x2412, refreshes at 120Hz and supports HDR10+, making it one of the more colour-rich displays in this group. An optical under-display fingerprint reader, NFC and Bluetooth 5.3 are on board, while a headphone jack is not. The listed configuration offers 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage.

A 6nm MediaTek Dimensity 7050 provides 5G performance, and the record says it handles PUBG Mobile and COD Mobile at smooth settings, with the 120Hz screen keeping visuals fluid. Heavier play in Genshin Impact may need medium graphics for stable frame rates. Cameras comprise a 64MP main unit, an 8MP ultrawide and a 2MP macro at the back, plus a 32MP front camera. No OIS or telephoto is listed, so stabilisation and zoom are not strengths to count on. The 5,000mAh battery charges at 67W, a clear practical advantage when you need power back quickly.

It runs Android 14 with ColorOS 14, and the record states no published update commitment in years for either OS or security patches. Buy it for the vibrant HDR display, fast charging and capable selfie camera at a market price below the official tag. Skip it if you want OIS, serious zoom, or a clearly stated long-term update policy.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 7050 handles PUBG Mobile and COD Mobile at smooth settings. The 120Hz AMOLED display gives fluid visuals, though heavy Genshin Impact sessions may need medium graphics for stable framerates.

Our Verdict

Positioned as a premium mid-range phone, the Oppo Reno 11 F 5G puts smooth 120Hz AMOLED, 1B colors, 120Hz, HDR10+ display that feels fast in everyday use at the top of its pitch. Headline hardware: the MediaTek Dimensity 7050 (6 nm) chipset and a 5,000mAh battery with 67W charging. Shortlist it if the spec mix suits you — if you can overlook one thing: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

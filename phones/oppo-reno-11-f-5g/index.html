Overview

The Oppo Reno 11 F 5G is a slim mid-range smartphone with a 6.7-inch 120Hz AMOLED display, MediaTek Dimensity 7050 chipset, and a 64MP triple camera. Its 5000mAh battery with 67W fast charging and lightweight 177g design make it a solid pick for style-conscious users wanting 5G on a budget.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 7050 handles PUBG Mobile and COD Mobile at smooth settings. The 120Hz AMOLED display gives fluid visuals, though heavy Genshin Impact sessions may need medium graphics for stable framerates.

Overview

The Oppo Reno 11 F 5G is a slim mid-range smartphone with a 6.7-inch 120Hz AMOLED display, MediaTek Dimensity 7050 chipset, and a 64MP triple camera. Its 5000mAh battery with 67W fast charging and lightweight 177g design make it a solid pick for style-conscious users wanting 5G on a budget.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 7050 handles PUBG Mobile and COD Mobile at smooth settings. The 120Hz AMOLED display gives fluid visuals, though heavy Genshin Impact sessions may need medium graphics for stable framerates.

What is the Oppo Reno 11 F 5G price in Pakistan?

Is the Oppo Reno 11 F 5G PTA approved?

Yes — units of the Oppo Reno 11 F 5G sold through official Oppo channels in Pakistan are PTA approved with the tax already paid. If you buy a non-official imported unit instead, check its IMEI on the DIRBS system before paying.

How is the Oppo Reno 11 F 5G battery life?

The Oppo Reno 11 F 5G carries a 5,000mAh battery with 67W fast charging. That is a large cell for this class, and most users should comfortably get a full day — heavier users with long gaming or video sessions may still charge overnight.

How good is the Oppo Reno 11 F 5G camera?

The rear setup is 64MP + 8MP (ultrawide) + 2MP (macro), and the front camera is 32MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the MediaTek Dimensity 7050 (6 nm).

How long will the Oppo Reno 11 F 5G get software updates?

Oppo has not published an update promise for this model.

What are the Oppo Reno 11 F 5G variants in Pakistan?

The variant tracked in Pakistan is 8GB RAM with 256GB storage. If a shop offers a different variant, it is likely an imported unit — confirm the warranty and PTA status for that specific unit.

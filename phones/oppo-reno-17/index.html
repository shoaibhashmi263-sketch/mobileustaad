Overview

The Oppo Reno 17 launched in China on November 16, 2026, as the larger phone in the new Reno 17 series — a deliberate swap from last year, when the vanilla Reno was the compact one. Its 6.56-inch 1.5K LTPS OLED display (2624x1208, 120Hz, 1,800 nits peak, Crystal Shield Glass) is flat and bright, and the 8,000mAh battery is the largest Oppo has ever fitted to a Reno, paired with 80W SUPERVOOC charging. Power comes from the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 (4nm), with 8GB or 12GB of LPDDR5X RAM and 256GB to 1TB of UFS 3.1 storage. The camera system is four 50MP sensors: a Sony main with OIS, an ultrawide with autofocus and a 116-degree field of view, a 3.5x periscope telephoto with OIS, and a 50MP autofocus selfie camera — the same quad-50MP setup Oppo confirmed ahead of launch. Popular games run smoothly at high settings, and Oppo adds 120fps gaming support. On the software side it runs Android 17 with ColorOS 17, in an IP69K-rated metal-frame body with an optical in-display fingerprint reader, NFC, an IR blaster and a dedicated AI button. It is aimed at buyers who want a big battery and a versatile camera system in a slim phone. No Pakistan price has been published yet — the phone is China-only for now, with sales starting November 20, 2026 — so we list no price rather than guess.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 is a powerful mid-range chip — heavy games run smoothly on high settings, and Oppo adds 120fps gaming support. The 8,000mAh battery makes long gaming sessions easy too.

Our Verdict

The Oppo Reno 17 arrives as a premium mid-range phone whose headline strength is smooth 120Hz LTPS OLED (2624x1208, 120Hz, 1,800 nits peak, Crystal Shield Glass) display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 (4nm; octa-core, Adreno 722) chipset and a 8,000mAh battery with 80W charging. On the software side it runs Android 17 (ColorOS 17). It is one of the more rounded options in its bracket — the one thing to accept first is this: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

Overview

The Oukitel WP63 is a rugged phone built for the outdoors, and its headline feature is genuinely unusual: it is the world's first rugged phone with a built-in electric igniter, so you can light a campfire even with no other fire-starting tools. Its 6.67-inch IPS LCD runs at 120Hz, though the 720 x 1600 resolution is low for a screen of this size, and the panel is protected by Gorilla Glass 5. Power comes from the Unisoc T8200, a 6nm 5G chip, with 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage (expandable by microSD). The rear camera is a 64MP main sensor with autofocus plus an 8MP night-vision camera with infrared lights, and the 20MP front camera handles selfies. The battery is the other headline: a 20,000mAh cell, the largest in its class, charged at 33W and able to reverse-charge other devices at 18W through a built-in Type-C cable. On the software side it runs Android 16, and Oukitel has not published an update policy for it. It is aimed at campers, hikers and outdoor workers who value battery life and toughness over slimness — at 629g and 27mm thick it is heavy, but it is IP68/IP69K rated and MIL-STD-810H tested. In Pakistan it has no official launch, so it is available only as a grey-market import at around Rs 93,999 (converted from the US $339 official store price) — confirm the final price, variant and PTA status with the seller before paying.

Gaming review

The Unisoc T8200 handles casual games, but demanding titles need low settings on the 720p display. The 20,000mAh battery is the real gaming asset — it lasts for days of play.

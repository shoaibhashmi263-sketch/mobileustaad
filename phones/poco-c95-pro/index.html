Overview

The Poco C95 Pro is Poco's mid-range phone, released in 2026. Its 6.9-inch LCD display runs at 120Hz, so scrolling, animations and supported games feel noticeably smoother than on a standard 60Hz panel.

Power comes from the MediaTek Helio G91 Ultra, which is aimed at everyday use — calls, social media, video and light gaming all run comfortably. The rear camera setup is 50 MP (main) + 2 MP, and the high-resolution main sensor is the one you will lean on for daylight shots, which come out detailed. Battery capacity is 7,500mAh — well above average, and the kind of size that can stretch to two days for lighter users, and 45W charging means top-ups are genuinely quick when you are in a hurry.

On the software side it runs Android 16 (HyperOS 3). It is aimed at buyers stepping up from an older phone who want a modern, balanced package.

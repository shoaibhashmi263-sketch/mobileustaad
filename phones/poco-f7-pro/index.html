Overview

The Poco F7 Pro is Poco's flagship-grade phone, released in 2025. Its 6.67-inch AMOLED display runs at 120Hz, so scrolling, animations and supported games feel noticeably smoother than on a standard 60Hz panel.

Power comes from the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, which delivers flagship-class speed, and popular games run at high settings without breaking a sweat. The rear camera setup is 50MP main + 8MP ultrawide, and the high-resolution main sensor is the one you will lean on for daylight shots, which come out detailed. Battery capacity is 6,000mAh — well above average, and the kind of size that can stretch to two days for lighter users, and 90W charging means top-ups are genuinely quick when you are in a hurry.

On the software side it runs Android 15. It is aimed at buyers who want premium features without paying full flagship money. In Pakistan it is listed at Rs 185,999 (official price, checked 2026-10-25) — confirm the final price, variant and warranty with the seller before paying.

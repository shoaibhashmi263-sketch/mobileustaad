Overview

The Poco X8 Power launched on 4 September 2026 as a battery-first performance phone, built around the Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 chipset with 8GB of RAM. Its 6.83-inch AMOLED display runs at 120Hz with 1280x2772 resolution, 3840Hz PWM dimming, HDR10+ and Dolby Vision, and a 3,500-nit peak, protected by Gorilla Glass Victus 2. Photography is led by a 50MP main camera with optical image stabilisation, joined by an 8MP ultrawide, while a 32MP front camera records 4K video. The headline feature is the 10,000mAh silicon-carbon battery, which charges at 100W wired with 27W reverse wired charging and bypass charging that powers the phone directly while gaming. It runs Android 16 with HyperOS 3, with up to four major Android upgrades promised, in a tough 229g body rated IP68/IP69K and drop-resistant to 3 metres, with stereo speakers, an IR blaster and an under-display optical fingerprint sensor. It has not been officially launched in Pakistan and reaches the market through importers; the dealer-confirmed price is Rs 114,999.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 with a 120Hz AMOLED display, a 10,000mAh battery, 100W charging and bypass charging makes this a strong midrange gaming phone — long sessions at high settings without heat or battery anxiety.

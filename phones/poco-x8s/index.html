Overview

The Poco X8s launched in Vietnam on October 21, 2026 as the compact fifth member of the Poco X8 series. It pairs the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 with a 6.35-inch 1.5K LTPO AMOLED (120Hz, 3,500-nit peak) and a triple camera led by a 50MP Light Fusion 800 main sensor with OIS plus a 50MP 2.5x telephoto. The 6,330mAh battery charges at 100W with 22.5W reverse charging, and the phone carries an IP68 rating, metal frame, Wi-Fi 7 and HyperOS 3 on Android 16. Colors: White, Black, Blue.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 with a 120Hz LTPO OLED is a strong upper-midrange gaming combo — smooth frame rates in demanding titles, though there is no active cooling or dedicated gaming hardware. (Editorial estimate, not a measured benchmark.)

Overview

The Poco X8s launched in Vietnam on October 21, 2026 as the compact fifth member of the Poco X8 series. It pairs the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 with a 6.35-inch 1.5K LTPO AMOLED (120Hz, 3,500-nit peak) and a triple camera led by a 50MP Light Fusion 800 main sensor with OIS plus a 50MP 2.5x telephoto. The 6,330mAh battery charges at 100W with 22.5W reverse charging, and the phone carries an IP68 rating, metal frame, Wi-Fi 7 and HyperOS 3 on Android 16. Colors: White, Black, Blue.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 with a 120Hz LTPO OLED is a strong upper-midrange gaming combo — smooth frame rates in demanding titles, though there is no active cooling or dedicated gaming hardware.

Price notes

Our Verdict

Positioned as a premium mid-range phone, the Poco X8s puts high-refresh 120Hz 1.5K LTPO AMOLED, flat (1220x2656, 3,500 nits peak, 12-bit, DCI-P3, 1.18mm bezels) display for smoother everyday use at the top of its pitch. It packs the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 (4nm) chipset and a 6,330mAh battery with 100W charging. It runs Android 16 (HyperOS 3) out of the box, with Poco promising 4 years of Android updates. Shortlist it if the spec mix suits you — the one thing to accept first is this: wired audio needs a usb-c adapter or dongle.

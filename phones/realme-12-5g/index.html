Overview

High megapixels lead the pitch for the Realme 12 5G, a model that has not been officially launched in Pakistan. The record gives an expected price of around Rs 63,999 from local price sites, alongside a market price of Rs 66,499 listed by PriceOye, for the configuration with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage.

A 6.72-inch IPS LCD provides 120Hz motion and a 1080 x 2400 resolution, giving it a sharpness advantage over HD+ rivals even if LCD contrast cannot match AMOLED. MediaTek's Dimensity 6100+ supplies 5G and everyday performance, and the gaming notes simply link results to that processor without awarding a rating, which suggests competence rather than a gaming specialism. Realme UI 5.0 runs over Android 14, unlocking is by a side-mounted fingerprint reader, and connectivity includes Bluetooth 5.2, USB-C and a welcome 3.5mm headphone jack, though NFC is absent.

The rear system pairs a 108MP wide camera with a 2MP depth helper, so detailed daylight captures are the promise, yet no stabilisation, ultrawide or telephoto hardware appears in the specification. An 8MP front camera handles selfies and video calls at a basic level. Inside, a 5,000mAh battery charges at 45W, matching the capacity and speed of several direct competitors. This suits buyers prioritising a detailed main sensor, a sharp fast screen and 5G; anyone wanting NFC or broader camera versatility should keep searching.

Our Verdict

With the Realme 12 5G, Realme is aiming at buyers who want high-refresh 120Hz IPS LCD display for smoother everyday use without paying flagship money. Headline hardware: the MediaTek Dimensity 6100+ chipset and a 5,000mAh battery with 45W charging. Buy it if the strengths above match how you use a phone; bearing in mind its clearest weakness: no promised android update period from realme for this model.

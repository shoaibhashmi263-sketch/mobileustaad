Overview

Sold in Pakistan since September 2026, the Realme C100 carries a market price of Rs 82,999 on PriceOye. Its 6.8-inch IPS LCD stretches HD+ resolution (720x1570) across a large canvas, so text and images will look less crisp than on FHD+ rivals, although the 120Hz refresh rate keeps scrolling fluid. Toughness is a selling point, with IP69 Pro water resistance, ArmorShell Glass and a claimed 2m drop resistance.

The Helio G92 Max, paired with 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage, is rated Good for gaming: GT Boost and Hyper Cooling help demanding games run at medium-high settings. A 50MP main camera with PDAF and an auxiliary lens handles rear photography, while the 8MP front camera is basic and best kept to well-lit selfies and calls; no ultrawide or stabilised main lens is listed. The 8,000mAh battery is built for marathon sessions and charges at 45W.

It runs Android 16 with realme UI 7.0, but no update commitment has been published. Practical touches include a headphone jack, NFC, a side-mounted fingerprint reader and a microSD slot shared with the SIM tray. At 219g it is heavy, and it is 4G only. Pick it for endurance, storage and durability on a budget; skip it if screen sharpness, 5G or a lighter handset matters more.

Our Verdict

Positioned as a premium mid-range phone, the Realme C100 puts fast 120Hz IPS LCD (HD+ 720x1570) screen, a clear step above 60Hz panels at the top of its pitch. Headline hardware: the MediaTek Helio G92 Max chipset and a 8,000mAh battery with 45W charging. It runs Android 16 (realme UI 7.0) out of the box. As a package it holds together well for the money — bearing in mind its clearest weakness: 4g only; there is no 5g support.

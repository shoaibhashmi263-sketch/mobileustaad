Overview

The Realme P4R 5G launched in India on June 10, 2026, as Realme's first budget phone with an 8,000mAh battery — a Titan silicon-carbon cell with 45W fast charging, bypass charging and reverse wired charging. Its 6.81-inch IPS LCD runs at 144Hz with up to 1,200 nits of high-brightness mode, though the HD+ (720x1570) resolution is modest for the size, and the panel is protected by Panda-1681 glass. Power comes from the MediaTek Dimensity 6300 (6nm), with 4GB or 6GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of UFS 2.2 storage (expandable via microSD). The rear camera is a single 50MP sensor (f/1.8, autofocus) and the front camera is 8MP — simple hardware, with the battery doing the heavy lifting. Light games run fine, but heavy titles need low settings. It runs Android 16 with Realme UI 7.0 in an IP65-rated body with MIL-STD-810H drop resistance, a side-mounted fingerprint reader and a nine-colour Pulse Light on the back that flashes for notifications and music. It is aimed at buyers who want multi-day battery life on a tight budget. No Pakistan price has been published yet — the phone launched in India only, starting at INR 18,999 — so we list no price rather than guess.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 6300 is an entry-level chip — light games run fine, but PUBG may lag on heavy settings. The 144Hz screen is a plus, though the HD+ panel is not the sharpest.

Our Verdict

For Realme, the Realme P4R 5G is a mid-range phone built around fast 144Hz IPS LCD (720x1570, 144Hz, 975 nits typ / 1,200 nits HBM, Panda-1681 glass) screen, a clear step above 60Hz panels. Headline hardware: the MediaTek Dimensity 6300 (6nm; octa-core 2x2.4GHz Cortex-A76 + 6x2.0GHz Cortex-A55, Mali-G57 MC2) chipset and a 8,000mAh battery with 45W charging. On the software side it runs Android 16 (Realme UI 7.0). Buyers in this segment should give it a serious look — the one thing to accept first is this: no headphone jack — wireless or usb-c audio only.

Overview

The Redmi 17 5G launched in the Philippines on October 23, 2026 as Xiaomi's budget 5G entry for the series. It pairs the 4nm Snapdragon 4 Gen 5 with a huge 6.9-inch 120Hz HD+ IPS display (Gorilla Glass 7i, IP64 dust/splash resistance, Wet Touch) and a 7,500mAh battery — 45W wired charging (full in ~96 minutes) plus 22.5W reverse wired charging to top up other devices. Cameras are a 50MP f/1.8 AI dual system with a customizable Dynamic RGB Light on the rear and an 8MP front camera, with dual stereo speakers (300% volume boost), side fingerprint, NFC, 3.5mm jack, microSD slot (up to 2TB) and HyperOS 3 on Android 16. Colors: Black, Deep Blue, Vivid Orange (textured PU leather back).

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 4 Gen 5 is a budget-tier 5G chip — smooth for casual games and daily play on the 120Hz display, but demanding titles need medium-low settings. The 7,500mAh battery is the real gaming asset: very long sessions without a charger. (Editorial estimate, not a measured benchmark.)

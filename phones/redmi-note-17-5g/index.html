Overview

Screen size and battery capacity dominate the Redmi Note 17 5G specification, a phone with no announced Pakistan launch at the time of the record. Its expected price of Rs 53,800 is calculated from the Chinese launch pricing, and the listed version pairs 6GB of RAM with a generous 256GB of storage.

Few phones at this level offer a display as large as the 6.99-inch OLED fitted here, which combines Full HD+ resolution, a 120Hz refresh rate, 1,800-nit peak brightness and Gorilla Glass 7i protection. Performance comes from the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4, rated Fair for gaming: it is an entry-level chip, adequate for casual and light games at modest settings, whereas heavy 3D titles require low settings, even if the big screen and battery suit long casual sessions. Android 16 runs with HyperOS 3 and medium bloatware, and the software notes cite a promise of up to three OS updates.

Only one rear camera is provided, a 50MP unit with an f/1.8 lens and PDAF that records video up to 1080p at 30 frames per second, and the front camera on this variant is 8MP. There is no ultrawide, telephoto or stabilisation listed, and no headphone jack either, although NFC and an infrared blaster are included. An 8,000mAh battery with 45W charging, plus 22.5W reverse wired charging, is exceptional, but the handset weighs 225g. It fits buyers wanting maximum screen and endurance; performance-focused gamers should look higher.

Gaming review

Our Verdict

Positioned as a mid-range phone, the Redmi Note 17 5G puts 120Hz OLED (2396x1080 FHD+, 120Hz, 1800 nits peak, 960Hz PWM, Gorilla Glass 7i) panel keeps scrolling and animations fluid at the top of its pitch. Headline hardware: the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 (4nm, 2x2.3GHz Cortex-A78 + 6x2.0GHz Cortex-A55, Adreno 613) chipset and a 8,000mAh battery with 45W charging. Software is Android 16 (HyperOS 3) as listed by Redmi. It is one of the more rounded options in its bracket — the one thing to accept first is this: no headphone jack — wireless or usb-c audio only.

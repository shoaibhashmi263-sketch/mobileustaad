Overview

The Redmi Note 17 Pro Refreshed Edition launched in China on October 13, 2026 as a China-only update to the Note 17 Pro 5G, in new Sand Orange and Energetic Green colors. The headline change is a 9,000mAh Si-C battery (up from 8,340mAh on the global model) with 67W wired and 22.5W reverse charging. The rest of the sheet carries over: Snapdragon 6s Gen 4, a 6.83-inch 120Hz 1.5K OLED, 50MP OIS main + 8MP ultrawide cameras, 16MP front camera, IP66/IP68/IP69/IP69K dust/water resistance, and Android 16 with HyperOS 3. China launch prices are CNY 1,599–1,699.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 with a 120Hz adaptive OLED and a huge 9,000mAh battery make this a strong mid-range gaming pick with all-day sessions; IP69-rated build adds durability. 67W charging refills quickly.

Our Verdict

The Xiaomi Redmi Note 17 Pro Refreshed Edition is a phone from Xiaomi, and its strongest suit is clear: smooth 120Hz flat OLED (1.5K 2772x1280, 10-bit, 3,500 nits peak, 3,840Hz PWM + DC-like dimming, DCI-P3, Gorilla Glass Victus 2) display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the Qualcomm Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 (4nm; 4x2.4GHz Cortex-A78 + 4x1.8GHz Cortex-A55, Adreno) chipset and a 9,000mAh battery with 67W charging. On the software side it runs Android 16. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

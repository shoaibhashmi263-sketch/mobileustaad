Overview

The Redmi R80m 5G appeared on Xiaomi Mall in China on 20 November 2026 alongside the R80 5G, sharing the same design and specifications but sold only in 6GB RAM configurations. It has a large 6.9-inch HD+ LCD display running at 120Hz and runs on the Dimensity 6300 chip. Photography is a single 13MP rear camera with a 5MP front camera for selfies. The 6,000mAh battery supports 33W wired charging. It ships with HyperOS 3 in a 205g body. The China listing price starts at CNY 2,199 for 6GB/128GB, with no sale date announced yet; no Pakistan launch or price has been announced, so the listed price is an expected price converted from the China listing price.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 6300 handles light games at low settings. This is a budget phone — not built for heavy gaming.

Our Verdict

With the Redmi R80m 5G, Redmi is aiming at buyers who want smooth 120Hz LCD (1600x720, 120Hz, 240Hz touch sampling) display that feels fast in everyday use without paying flagship money. It packs the Dimensity 6300 chipset and a 6,000mAh battery with 33W charging. Software is HyperOS 3 as listed by Redmi. As a package it holds together well for the money — bearing in mind its clearest weakness: no headphone jack — wireless or usb-c audio only.

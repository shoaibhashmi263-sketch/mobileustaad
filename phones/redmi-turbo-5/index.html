Overview

The Redmi Turbo 5 launched in China on 29 January 2026 as a performance-focused midrange phone, built around the MediaTek Dimensity 8500 Ultra chipset with up to 16GB of RAM. Its 6.59-inch AMOLED display runs at 120Hz with 1268x2756 resolution, 3840Hz PWM dimming, a 3,500-nit peak and Dolby Vision support, so games and video look sharp and smooth. Photography is led by a 50MP Sony IMX882 main camera with optical image stabilisation, joined by an 8MP ultrawide, while a 20MP front camera handles selfies. The 7,560mAh battery is unusually large for the class and charges at 100W wired, with 27W reverse wired charging for topping up other devices. It runs Android 16 with HyperOS 3, with up to four major Android upgrades promised, in a metal-frame, glass-back body rated IP68/IP69K. It has not been officially launched in Pakistan and reaches the market through importers; the expected price is Rs 99,999 for the 12GB/256GB variant.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 8500 Ultra with a 120Hz AMOLED display and a 7,560mAh battery handles demanding games at high settings, and 100W charging tops the big battery up quickly; this is a performance-first midrange phone rather than a dedicated gaming flagship.

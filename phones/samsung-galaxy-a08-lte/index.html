Our Verdict

The Samsung Galaxy A08 LTE is a budget phone from Samsung, and its strongest suit is clear: large 6.7-inch PLS LCD (HD+ 720x1600) display. It packs the MediaTek Helio G99+ (6nm) chipset and a 6,000mAh battery with 25W charging. On the software side it runs Android 17 (One UI 9), and Samsung promises 6 years of Android updates. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no 5g support — 4g only.

How good is the Samsung Galaxy A08 LTE camera?

The rear setup is 50 MP (f/1.8) + 2 MP (depth), and the front camera is 8MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the MediaTek Helio G99+ (6nm).

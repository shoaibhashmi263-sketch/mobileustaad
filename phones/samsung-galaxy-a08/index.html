Overview

The Samsung Galaxy A08 went on sale in Pakistan on 19 November 2026 as an affordable 4G phone with a 6.7-inch HD+ PLS LCD display running at 90Hz with Gorilla Glass 5 protection. It runs on the Helio G99+ chip with 4GB of RAM and 64GB of storage in the base model, expandable by microSD. Photography is led by a 50MP main camera joined by a 2MP depth sensor, while an 8MP front camera handles selfies. The 6,000mAh battery supports 25W wired charging. It runs One UI 9 on Android 17 with 6 years of OS and security updates promised, in a body rated IP64. The official Pakistan price starts at Rs 55,999 for 4GB/64GB (4GB/128GB Rs 67,999; 6GB/128GB Rs 78,999).

Gaming review

The Helio G99+ runs PUBG fine on medium settings. Good for daily casual gaming, not for heavy games.

Our Verdict

Positioned as a mid-range phone, the Samsung Galaxy A08 puts roomy 6.7-inch PLS LCD (HD+, 90Hz, Gorilla Glass 5) panel at the top of its pitch. It packs the Helio G99+ chipset and a 6,000mAh battery with 25W charging. On the software side it runs Android 17 (One UI 9), and Samsung promises 6 years of Android updates. It deserves a place on the shortlist in this range — bearing in mind its clearest weakness: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

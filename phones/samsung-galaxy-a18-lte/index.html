Overview

The Samsung Galaxy A18 LTE launched in Pakistan on 19 November 2026 as Samsung's step-up budget phone, with a 6.7-inch FHD+ Super AMOLED display at 90Hz protected by Gorilla Glass 3. The slim 7.7mm body comes in Black, Silver and Light Blue, with IP64 dust and splash protection. The Pakistan variant runs the MediaTek Helio G99+ (the European version uses Samsung's Exynos 1610 instead), paired with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage — an 8GB/256GB version is also sold — plus a microSD slot expandable to 2TB. The rear camera setup is a 50MP main (f/1.8, autofocus, OIS) + 5MP ultrawide + 2MP macro, and the 13MP front camera handles selfies. The 5,000mAh battery charges at 25W, reaching 50% in about 30 minutes. On the software side it runs Android 17 with One UI 9, and Samsung promises 6 generations of OS upgrades plus 6 years of security updates, so it stays current far longer than most rivals at this price. NFC, Knox Vault and Circle to Search are included; audio goes through USB-C, as there is no 3.5mm jack. It is aimed at buyers who want an AMOLED screen and long software support without crossing Rs 100,000. In Pakistan the official price is Rs 99,999 for 8GB/128GB (8GB/256GB Rs 122,500).

Gaming review

A budget chipset and 90Hz AMOLED keep casual games smooth, but this is a longevity-first phone, not a gaming one.

Our Verdict

With the Samsung Galaxy A18 LTE, Samsung is aiming at buyers who want big 6.7-inch FHD+ Super AMOLED (1080x2340, 90Hz, Gorilla Glass 3) screen for video and reading without paying flagship money. It packs the MediaTek Helio G99+ (6nm) chipset and a 5,000mAh battery with 25W charging. On the software side it runs Android 17 (One UI 9), and Samsung promises 6 years of Android updates. Overall it is a sound buy in its segment — with its main drawback understood upfront: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

How is the Samsung Galaxy A18 LTE battery life?

The Samsung Galaxy A18 LTE carries a 5,000mAh battery with 25W fast charging. The capacity is firmly in large-battery territory for the segment — a full day of typical use should be comfortable, with heavy gaming or video days still ending on a top-up.

How good is the Samsung Galaxy A18 LTE camera?

Cameras are 50MP main (f/1.8, AF, OIS) + 5MP ultrawide (f/2.2) + 2MP macro (f/2.4) at the back and 13MP at the front. It is a solid paper specification for a phone at this price, expect its best results in daylight; night output depends on the MediaTek Helio G99+ (6nm)'s image processing.

Overview

The Samsung Galaxy F70 5G launched in India on October 18, 2026 as an India-exclusive budget 5G phone — a rebranded Galaxy A17 5G. It pairs a 6.7-inch FHD+ Super AMOLED display with Gorilla Glass Victus, Samsung's 5nm Exynos 1330 chip, a 50MP OIS 'No Shake Cam' triple rear camera, a 5,000mAh battery with 25W charging, and a slim 7.5mm body with IP54 protection, running Android 15 with One UI 7.0. India pricing starts at INR 19,999 (4GB/128GB); a Pakistan launch has not been announced.

Gaming review

The Exynos 1330 with a 90Hz AMOLED handles casual and mid-weight games fine at medium settings, but it is not a gaming chip — heavy titles need lowered settings and the 25W charging refills the 5,000mAh battery slowly by 2026 standards. (Editorial estimate, not a measured benchmark.)

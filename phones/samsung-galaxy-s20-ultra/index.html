Overview

Few phones of its era were as maximal as the Galaxy S20 Ultra, and it is now discontinued: the Rs 220,000 figure attached to it is the last known official price per WhatMobile, not a current tag. The front is dominated by a 6.9-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X panel at 1440 x 3200 with a 120Hz refresh rate, still a big, sharp canvas for video and games. An ultrasonic fingerprint reader lives under the display, and you get 5G, NFC and USB-C 3.2, though no headphone jack.

An Exynos 990 with 12GB of RAM powers this variant, and the gaming assessment calls it the best S20 for play: heavy titles run fluidly and the battery holds up well, with some heat appearing only under marathon sessions. Storage is 128GB. The rear camera array combines 108MP, 48MP and 12MP sensors with a 0.3MP time-of-flight unit for depth sensing — the 48MP lens gives it real reach for zoomed shots — while selfies come from a 40MP front camera.

Its 5,000mAh battery charges at 45W, quicker than many newer Samsung models manage. The record credits it with three OS updates and four years of security updates from its Android 10 launch software, a window that reflects its 2020 release. Consider it only if you find a well-kept unit and value screen size and camera hardware over software freshness; buyers wanting current updates or warranty-backed purchases should move to a newer model.

Gaming review

With 12GB RAM, a 6.9-inch 120Hz display and the largest battery of the series, the S20 Ultra is the best S20 for gaming — heavy titles run fluidly and battery life is excellent. The Exynos 990 still shows some heat under marathon sessions, but performance headroom is generous for a 2020 phone.

Our Verdict

Headline hardware: the Exynos 990 (7 nm+) chipset and a 5,000mAh battery with 45W charging. It is one of the more rounded options in its bracket — with one caveat to weigh first: wired audio needs a usb-c adapter or dongle.

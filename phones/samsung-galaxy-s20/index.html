Overview

The Samsung Galaxy S20 is Samsung's flagship-grade phone, released in 2020. Its 6.2-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz display runs at 120Hz, so scrolling, animations and supported games feel noticeably smoother than on a standard 60Hz panel.

Power comes from the Exynos 990 (7 nm+), which drives the phone's everyday performance, working alongside the RAM and storage on offer. The rear camera setup is 64MP + 12MP + 12MP, and the high-resolution main sensor is the one you will lean on for daylight shots, which come out detailed. Battery capacity is 4,000mAh, and 25W charging means top-ups are reasonably brisk when you are in a hurry.

It is aimed at buyers who want premium features without paying full flagship money. In Pakistan it is listed at Rs 170,999 (official price, checked 2026-10-25) — confirm the final price, variant and warranty with the seller before paying.

Gaming review

The Exynos 990 with Mali-G77 MP11 GPU handles popular titles like PUBG Mobile and COD Mobile at high settings with the 120Hz display keeping gameplay smooth, though the chip is known to run warm under sustained load. The compact 6.2-inch size and stereo speakers make it comfortable for gaming, but the 4000mAh battery drains quickly in heavy sessions.

Our Verdict

The Samsung Galaxy S20 is a flagship-grade phone that leads with smooth 120Hz Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the Exynos 990 (7 nm+) chipset and a 4,000mAh battery with 25W charging. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

How is the Samsung Galaxy S20 battery life?

The Samsung Galaxy S20 carries a 4,000mAh battery with 25W fast charging. Most users should get through a typical day of calls, social media and video; heavy gaming will drain it faster.

How good is the Samsung Galaxy S20 camera?

The rear setup is 64MP + 12MP + 12MP, and the front camera is 10MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Exynos 990 (7 nm+).

How long will the Samsung Galaxy S20 get software updates?

Samsung promises 3 years of Android updates and 4 years of security patches for this model.

What are the Samsung Galaxy S20 variants in Pakistan?

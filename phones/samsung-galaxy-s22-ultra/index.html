Overview

Samsung's 2022 range-topper still presents a heavyweight specification, anchored by a 6.8-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display running at 120Hz. Its official price in Pakistan is Rs 424,999 according to WhatMobile, while the recorded market price sits much lower at Rs 244,999. Power comes from the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 built on a 4 nm process, matched with 12GB of RAM, 256GB of storage, 5G connectivity, NFC and Bluetooth 5.2, and unlocking is via an ultrasonic fingerprint sensor beneath the screen.

For gaming, the record describes the sharp QHD+ 120Hz panel and the Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 delivering the smoothest experience within the S22 family, with the larger body shedding heat better than its smaller siblings, even if lower graphics settings still help during extended play. The camera system lists four rear lenses — 108MP, 12MP, 10MP and 10MP — giving wide, ultrawide and zoom coverage, while the 40MP front camera is unusually high-resolution for selfies. Buyers should judge the zoom claims by the listed hardware alone, since the record gives resolutions rather than detailed lens roles beyond the count.

A 5,000mAh battery with 45W charging keeps the phone going through long sessions, per the gaming notes, though there is no headphone jack. It launched on Android 12 with One UI 4.1. This suits buyers wanting a big, sharp screen, a versatile multi-lens rear setup and strong gaming credentials at the lower market figure; those who prioritise the freshest chipset generation or wired audio should look elsewhere.

Gaming review

The large QHD+ 120Hz display and Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 deliver the sharpest, smoothest mobile gaming experience of the S22 family, with the 5000mAh battery lasting through long sessions. The bigger chassis dissipates heat better than its smaller siblings, though it still benefits from lower graphics settings for extended play.

Our Verdict

The Samsung Galaxy S22 Ultra is a flagship-grade phone that leads with smooth 120Hz Dynamic AMOLED 2X display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the Qualcomm SM8450 Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 (4 nm) chipset and a 5,000mAh battery with 45W charging. Buy it if the strengths above match how you use a phone; look elsewhere if this matters to you: wired audio needs a usb-c adapter or dongle.

How is the Samsung Galaxy S22 Ultra battery life?

The Samsung Galaxy S22 Ultra carries a 5,000mAh battery with 45W fast charging. At 5,000mAh it sits near the top of its class for capacity, most users will see a full day, helped by 45W charging when a top-up is needed.

How good is the Samsung Galaxy S22 Ultra camera?

It pairs 108MP + 12MP + 10MP + 10MP on the rear with a 40MP selfie camera. For this price class, the camera hardware is well specified, daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Qualcomm SM8450 Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 (4 nm).

Overview

The Galaxy S22 is the compact member of Samsung's 2022 flagship family, and in Pakistan its official price is Rs 232,999 per WhatMobile for the 8GB/256GB version, against a market price of Rs 210,299. Its 6.1-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X screen runs at 120Hz, giving a smaller phone the same motion smoothness as its bigger siblings. Unlocking is handled by an ultrasonic in-display fingerprint sensor, and connectivity includes 5G, NFC, Bluetooth 5.2 and USB-C 3.2, but no headphone jack.

Power comes from the Snapdragon 8 Gen 1, which runs PUBG Mobile and Genshin Impact at high settings without trouble in shorter bursts. The honest caveat sits in the gaming notes: the 3,700mAh battery is small and the 4nm chip runs warm, so marathon sessions drain power quickly and can trigger throttling. Cameras are a 50MP main, a 10MP unit and a 12MP unit on the rear — a three-lens spread that covers everyday shooting and modest zoom needs — with a 10MP front camera that is fine for calls and casual selfies rather than a headline feature.

Charging is 25W, which at least suits the small battery it has to fill. The phone launched on Android 12 with One UI 4.1, and no update figures are published in its record. This is a pick for people who specifically want a pocket-friendly Samsung flagship and accept shorter endurance as the cost; heavy gamers and all-day heavy users should look at larger-battery alternatives instead.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 with Adreno 730 runs demanding titles like PUBG Mobile and Genshin Impact at high settings, and the 120Hz AMOLED display keeps motion smooth. The compact 3700mAh battery and heat-prone 4nm chip mean long sessions drain power quickly and may throttle, so a fast 25W top-up between games helps.

Our Verdict

For Samsung, the Samsung Galaxy S22 is a flagship-grade phone built around high-refresh 120Hz Dynamic AMOLED 2X display for smoother everyday use. Headline hardware: the Qualcomm SM8450 Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 (4 nm) chipset and a 3,700mAh battery with 25W charging. Buyers in this segment should give it a serious look — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

Is the Samsung Galaxy S22 PTA approved?

Yes — units of the Samsung Galaxy S22 sold through official Samsung channels in Pakistan are PTA approved with the tax already paid. For a non-official import bought elsewhere, run a DIRBS check on that unit's IMEI first.

How is the Samsung Galaxy S22 battery life?

The Samsung Galaxy S22 carries a 3,700mAh battery with 25W fast charging. Moderate users should manage a full day on a charge, but plan a top-up if you game for hours.

How good is the Samsung Galaxy S22 camera?

Cameras are 50MP + 10MP + 12MP at the back and 10MP at the front. Judged on hardware, it stands up well against its rivals, expect its best results in daylight; night output depends on the Qualcomm SM8450 Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 (4 nm)'s image processing.

How long will the Samsung Galaxy S22 get software updates?

For the Samsung Galaxy S22, Samsung has not published an update commitment.

What are the Samsung Galaxy S22 variants in Pakistan?

The variant tracked in Pakistan is 8GB RAM with 256GB storage. A seller offering different memory is most likely holding imported stock, ask who honours the warranty on that unit and whether it is PTA approved.

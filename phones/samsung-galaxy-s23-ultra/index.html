Overview

The Samsung Galaxy S23 Ultra is Samsung's flagship-grade phone, released in 2023. Its 6.8-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz, HDR10+ display runs at 120Hz, so scrolling, animations and supported games feel noticeably smoother than on a standard 60Hz panel.

Power comes from the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy (4 nm), which drives the phone's everyday performance, working alongside the RAM and storage on offer. The rear camera setup is 200MP + 12MP + 10MP + 10MP, and the high-resolution main sensor is the one you will lean on for daylight shots, which come out detailed. Battery capacity is 5,000mAh, which is a full-day size for most users, and 45W charging means top-ups are genuinely quick when you are in a hurry.

It is aimed at buyers who want premium features without paying full flagship money. In Pakistan it is listed at Rs 389,999 (official price, checked 2026-10-25) — confirm the final price, variant and warranty with the seller before paying.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy with its overclocked GPU is a gaming monster. The huge 6.8-inch display, stereo speakers, and 5000mAh battery deliver a true flagship gaming experience.

Our Verdict

The Samsung Galaxy S23 Ultra is a flagship-grade phone that leads with smooth 120Hz Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz, HDR10+ display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy (4 nm) chipset and a 5,000mAh battery with 45W charging. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

How good is the Samsung Galaxy S23 Ultra camera?

The rear setup is 200MP + 12MP + 10MP + 10MP, and the front camera is 12MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy (4 nm).

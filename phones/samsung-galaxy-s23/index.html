Overview

Compact flagship buyers are the natural audience here, and the Galaxy S23 lists an official price of Rs 399,999 for a phone built around a 6.1-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display. That screen offers 120Hz, HDR10+ and a 2340 x 1080 resolution, so it stays sharp and fluid despite the smaller footprint. Performance comes from the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy, matched with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage.

Gaming notes credit the chip with top-tier results and excellent thermal efficiency, running PUBG Mobile and Genshin Impact at maximum settings smoothly on the 120Hz panel. Three rear cameras are listed at 50MP, 12MP and 10MP, giving more framing options than a basic dual setup, and the front camera is 12MP. The trade-off for the small body is a 3,900mAh battery with 25W charging, the slowest wired speed among the Samsung flagships in this batch. An ultrasonic under-display fingerprint reader, 5G, NFC, Bluetooth 5.3 and USB Type-C 3.2 complete the specification.

Software support is stated plainly in the record: four years of OS updates and five years of security updates, starting from Android 13 with One UI 5.1. It fits people who want genuine flagship speed in a phone that disappears into a pocket. Heavy users who drain a battery before evening, or anyone needing generous built-in storage, should think twice.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy delivers top-tier gaming with excellent thermal efficiency. PUBG Mobile and Genshin Impact run at max settings smoothly on the 120Hz display.

Our Verdict

The Samsung Galaxy S23 is one of Samsung's premium offerings, led by smooth 120Hz Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 120Hz, HDR10+ display that feels fast in everyday use. Headline hardware: the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy (4 nm) chipset and a 3,900mAh battery with 25W charging. It deserves a place on the shortlist in this range — the one thing to accept first is this: the 3.5mm headphone jack is missing.

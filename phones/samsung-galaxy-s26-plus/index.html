Overview

The Samsung Galaxy S26+ is Samsung's flagship-grade phone, released in 2026. Its 6.7-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X (LTPO, QHD+ 3120x1440, 1-120Hz adaptive, HDR10+, ~2,600 nits peak, Gorilla Glass Victus 2, Armor Aluminum 2 frame) display runs at 120Hz, so scrolling, animations and supported games feel noticeably smoother than on a standard 60Hz panel.

Power comes from the Samsung Exynos 2600 (2nm, deca-core up to 3.9 GHz, Xclipse 960 GPU) — Pakistan variant per Samsung regional split, which delivers flagship-class speed, and popular games run at high settings without breaking a sweat. The rear camera setup is 50 MP wide (f/1.8, 1/1.56", OIS) + 12 MP ultrawide (f/2.2, 120°) + 10 MP telephoto (3x optical, f/2.4, OIS); 8K@30fps video, and the high-resolution main sensor is the one you will lean on for daylight shots, which come out detailed. Battery capacity is 4,900mAh, and 45W charging means top-ups are genuinely quick when you are in a hurry.

On the software side it runs Android 16 (One UI 8.5), and Samsung promises 7 years of Android updates, so it stays current for longer than most rivals at this price. It is aimed at buyers who want premium features without paying full flagship money. In Pakistan it is listed at Rs 332,995 (market price, checked 2026-10-09) — confirm the final price, variant and warranty with the seller before paying.

Gaming review

The Exynos 2600 chews through demanding games at max settings, and the large 6.7-inch 1-120Hz QHD+ display plus the 4,900mAh battery make the S26+ a superb long-session gaming phone.

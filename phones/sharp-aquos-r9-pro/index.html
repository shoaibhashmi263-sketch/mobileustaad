Overview

The Sharp Aquos R9 Pro is Sharp's true 2025 flagship, with a 1-inch-type main sensor, triple 50.3MP Leica cameras and a 2.8x telephoto. Its 6.7-inch 1440p 240Hz IGZO OLED and Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 deliver top-tier performance, with 32W wired and 15W wireless charging. Like all Sharp phones, it reaches Pakistan only via importers.

Our Verdict

The Sharp Aquos R9 Pro is Sharp's love letter to mobile photographers: a Leica triple camera with a real 2.8x telephoto, a stunning 6.7-inch QHD+ 240Hz display and an ultrasonic fingerprint sensor — hardware that embarrasses phones costing more. At Rs 219,999 it undercuts every mainstream Ultra on camera versatility per rupee. Buy it if you are a camera enthusiast who values Leica color science and does not mind importing. Skip it if you want local warranty, Samsung/Apple-grade resale value, or the absolute fastest chip — at this price a Galaxy S24 Ultra with full Pakistani support is the safer mainstream pick.

FAQs

What is the Sharp Aquos R9 Pro price in Pakistan?

Around Rs 219,999 via importers (expected unofficial price — Sharp has no official Pakistan launch). Always verify PTA approval and the importer's return policy.

Does the Aquos R9 Pro have a telephoto camera?

Yes — unlike the standard R9, the Pro adds a 50.3MP telephoto with 2.8x optical zoom and OIS, all Leica-tuned. It is the main reason to choose the Pro.

Is the R9 Pro better than the Galaxy S24 Ultra?

On paper the R9 Pro matches much of the S24 Ultra's camera and display hardware for less money, but the S24 Ultra wins on Pakistani warranty, software updates, S Pen, and resale. Enthusiasts may prefer the R9 Pro's Leica look; most buyers are safer with Samsung.

What display does the Aquos R9 Pro have?

A 6.7-inch Pro IGZO LTPO OLED at 1440x3120 resolution with a 240Hz refresh rate and Dolby Vision — one of the sharpest and smoothest panels on any phone.

Should I worry about buying an import-only phone?

The main risks are no manufacturer warranty, uncertain PTA tax costs, and difficult repairs. If you accept those, importers offer genuine units — just buy from a reputable seller with a written warranty of their own.

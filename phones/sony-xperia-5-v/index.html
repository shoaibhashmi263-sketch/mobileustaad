What is the Sony Xperia 5 V price in Pakistan?

Is the Sony Xperia 5 V PTA approved?

The Sony Xperia 5 V is largely sold through market and import channels in Pakistan, so PTA status depends on the individual unit. Before paying, verify the IMEI via DIRBS (send the IMEI to 8484) and factor in the PTA tax if the unit is not already approved.

How is the Sony Xperia 5 V battery life?

The Sony Xperia 5 V carries a 5,000mAh battery with 30W fast charging. That is a large cell for this class, and most users should comfortably get a full day — heavier users with long gaming or video sessions may still charge overnight.

How good is the Sony Xperia 5 V camera?

The rear setup is 48MP wide (OIS) + 12MP ultrawide, ZEISS, and the front camera is 12MP. On paper that is a competitive setup for this price class; daylight photos are its strength, while low-light results depend on the processing of the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 (4 nm).

How long will the Sony Xperia 5 V get software updates?

Sony has not published an update promise for this model.

What are the Sony Xperia 5 V variants in Pakistan?

The variant tracked in Pakistan is 8GB RAM with 256GB storage. If a shop offers a different variant, it is likely an imported unit — confirm the warranty and PTA status for that specific unit.

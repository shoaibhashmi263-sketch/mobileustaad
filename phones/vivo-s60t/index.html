Overview

The vivo S60t pairs the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 with a 6.59-inch 144Hz 1.5K AMOLED, a 50MP periscope telephoto camera, a 50MP autofocus selfie camera and a 7,200mAh battery with 90W charging.

Gaming review

The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 is a strong upper-midrange gaming chip that runs demanding games at high settings on the 144Hz AMOLED.

Price notes

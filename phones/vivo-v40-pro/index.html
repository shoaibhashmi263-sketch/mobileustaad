Overview

The Vivo V40 Pro is Vivo's flagship-grade phone, released in 2026. Its 6.78-inch AMOLED display runs at 120Hz, so scrolling, animations and supported games feel noticeably smoother than on a standard 60Hz panel.

Power comes from the MediaTek Dimensity 9200+ (4 nm), which drives the phone's everyday performance, working alongside the RAM and storage on offer. The rear camera setup is 50MP wide (OIS, ZEISS) + 50MP telephoto (2x optical zoom, OIS) + 50MP ultrawide, and the high-resolution main sensor is the one you will lean on for daylight shots, which come out detailed. Battery capacity is 5,500mAh, which is a full-day size for most users, and 80W charging means top-ups are genuinely quick when you are in a hurry.

It is aimed at buyers who want premium features without paying full flagship money. In Pakistan it is listed at Rs 164,999 (expected price, checked 2026-10-17) — confirm the final price, variant and warranty with the seller before paying.

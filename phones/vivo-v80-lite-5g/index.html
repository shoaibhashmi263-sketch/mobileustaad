Overview

Do not let the Lite name fool you about battery size: the Vivo V80 Lite 5G packs a 10,000mAh cell. Its official price from vivo Pakistan is Rs 124,999, with PriceOye listing a market price of Rs 109,995, and the record's notes list Pakistani variants at 8GB/128GB for Rs 124,999 and 8GB/256GB for Rs 149,999. The display is a 6.83-inch AMOLED panel with a 120Hz refresh rate, paired here with 6GB of RAM and 128GB of storage.

MediaTek's Dimensity 7300e drives the phone and earns a Very good gaming rating, running demanding games at medium-high settings while the huge battery stretches play sessions considerably. Camera duties fall to a 50MP main sensor from Sony plus a 2MP secondary lens, which means no ultrawide for landscapes and no telephoto for zoom; the 32MP front camera is the more respectable half of the setup for selfies and calls.

Charging is 44W, which will take patience with a battery this large, so overnight charging suits it better than quick top-ups. It runs Android 16 with OriginOS 6, NFC and Bluetooth 5.4 are included, and vivo has made no Pakistan statement on update duration for this model. Choose it purely for endurance and a big, fast screen at an official local price; pass if camera versatility or rapid refuelling matters more.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 7300e is a strong midrange gaming chip that runs demanding games at medium-high settings; the huge battery means marathon sessions.

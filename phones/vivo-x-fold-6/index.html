Overview

The Vivo X Fold 6 is Vivo's phone, released in 2026. Its 8.02-inch Foldable LTPO AMOLED (2K+ 2504x2312, 5000 nits peak) + 6.51-inch FHD+ LTPO AMOLED cover (2528x1120) display runs at 120Hz, so scrolling, animations and supported games feel noticeably smoother than on a standard 60Hz panel.

Power comes from the MediaTek Dimensity 9500 Super Edition (3nm), which delivers flagship-class speed, and popular games run at high settings without breaking a sweat. The rear camera setup is 200 MP main (Samsung HPB, 1/1.4", f/1.68, OIS, ZEISS T*) + 50 MP 3x periscope telephoto (Sony LYT602, f/2.57, OIS, ZEISS APO) + 50 MP ultrawide (f/2.05); ZEISS co-engineered, 100x HyperZoom, 8K video, and the high-resolution main sensor is the one you will lean on for daylight shots, which come out detailed. Battery capacity is 6,900mAh — well above average, and the kind of size that can stretch to two days for lighter users, and 80W charging means top-ups are genuinely quick when you are in a hurry.

On the software side it runs Android 17 (OriginOS 7 Fold). It is aimed at buyers stepping up from an older phone who want a modern, balanced package. A confirmed Pakistan price has not been published yet, so we do not list one rather than guess; imported units may show up in local markets first.

Gaming review

The 3nm Dimensity 9500 Super is a flagship gaming chip; the huge foldable inner display and Mali-G1 Ultra GPU deliver top-tier performance with room to spare.

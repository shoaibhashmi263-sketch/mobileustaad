Overview

The Vivo X300 FE is a compact flagship for Pakistan at Rs 269,999, pairing the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 with ZEISS triple cameras (50MP main + 50MP telephoto + 8MP ultrawide) and a 50MP front camera. A 6.31-inch LTPO AMOLED, 6,500mAh BlueVolt battery with 90W + 40W wireless charging, and 5 years of OS updates make it the small-phone flagship to beat.

Our Verdict

The Vivo X300 FE is one of Vivo's premium offerings, led by smooth 120Hz LTPO AMOLED display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset and a 6,500mAh battery with 90W charging. On the software side it runs Android 16, and Vivo promises 5 years of Android updates. Buy it if the strengths above match how you use a phone; look elsewhere if this matters to you: no 3.5mm headphone jack.

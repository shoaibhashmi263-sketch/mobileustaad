Overview

The vivo X500 launched in China on September 21, 2026 as the base model of vivo's 2026 flagship series — and it is barely a compromise. It runs the 3nm Dimensity 9600M with 12GB of RAM, a 6.59-inch 1.5K 144Hz AMOLED, and a Zeiss triple camera: a 50MP Sony LYTIA 828 main, a 64MP 3x periscope telephoto and a 50MP ultrawide, plus a 50MP front camera. The 7,500mAh BlueVolt battery charges at 90W wired with 40W wireless, and the phone carries IP68/IP69 ratings. China prices start at CNY 5,499; there is no Pakistan launch or official PK price yet.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 9600M is flagship-class silicon and the 144Hz AMOLED is made for high-frame-rate play; the 7,500mAh battery outlasts most rivals. (Editorial estimate, not a measured benchmark.)

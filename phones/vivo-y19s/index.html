Overview

The Vivo Y19s is Vivo's budget phone, released in 2024. Its 6.68-inch IPS LCD display runs at 90Hz, a step up in smoothness from the basic 60Hz panels still common at this price.

Power comes from the Unisoc T612 (12 nm), which is aimed at everyday use — calls, social media, video and light gaming all run comfortably. The rear camera setup is 50 MP (wide) + auxiliary lens, and the high-resolution main sensor is the one you will lean on for daylight shots, which come out detailed. Battery capacity is 5,500mAh, which is a full-day size for most users, and 44W charging means top-ups are reasonably brisk when you are in a hurry.

On the software side it runs Android 15. It is aimed at buyers who want a dependable everyday phone without overspending. In Pakistan it is listed at Rs 42,999 (market price, checked 2026-09-30) — confirm the final price, variant and warranty with the seller before paying.

Gaming review

The Unisoc T612 is a budget chip — casual games run fine, play PUBG on low settings.

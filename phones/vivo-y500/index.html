Overview

The Vivo Y500 is a battery monster for Pakistan with an 8,100mAh cell, 44W charging and a 6.83-inch 1.5K AMOLED 120Hz display at Rs 99,999. The 50MP Sony IMX852 main camera, IP68/IP69 rating and MIL-STD-810H durability make it a tough all-rounder, though the Unisoc T7300 is 4G-only.

Gaming review

The Unisoc T7300 handles casual games and PUBG on low-medium settings; the huge battery is the real gaming advantage here — ~10 hours of PUBG per Vivo's claim. Not a performance phone. (Editorial estimate, not a measured benchmark.)

Our Verdict

The Vivo Y500 is a premium mid-range phone that leads with smooth 120Hz AMOLED display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the Unisoc T7300 chipset and a 8,100mAh battery with 44W charging. On the software side it runs Android 16. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: no 5g support — 4g only.

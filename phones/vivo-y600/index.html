Overview

The Vivo Y600 launched in China on 26 October 2026 as a battery-first mid-range phone. Its headline feature is a 10,000mAh battery, which Vivo says lasted 32 hours and 37 minutes in a continuous livestream test — a Guinness World Records title for the longest phone product test livestream. Sales in China begin on 30 October, and the phone is a rebrand of the vivo S2 FE / V80 Lite for the Chinese market. The 6.83-inch 1.5K AMOLED display runs at 120Hz with a 2,000-nit peak, and the MediaTek Dimensity 7300e keeps everyday use smooth. Photography comes from a 50MP Sony IMX852 main camera plus an 8MP selfie camera, while 44W wired charging (with reverse wired charging for topping up other devices) refills the huge battery. The phone is built tough, with IP68 and IP69 ratings and a MIL-STD-810H durability designation. On the software side it runs Android 16 with OriginOS 6 in China. It is aimed at buyers who value battery life above all else. No Pakistan price or launch has been announced yet — the China prices start at CNY 1,999 (6/128GB), rising to CNY 2,499 (8/256GB). Colours: Diamond Gold, Obsidian Black and Sapphire Blue.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 7300e with a 120Hz AMOLED display handles popular titles at sensible settings, but this is a battery-first phone, not a gaming phone — there is no gaming chip or active cooling.

Our Verdict

The Vivo Y600 is a premium mid-range phone from Vivo, and its strongest suit is clear: high-refresh 120Hz 1.5K AMOLED (2800x1260, 120Hz, HDR10+, 2,000 nits peak) display for smoother everyday use. It packs the MediaTek Dimensity 7300e (4nm, octa-core, Mali-G610) chipset and a 10,000mAh battery with 44W charging. On the software side it runs Android 16 (OriginOS 6, China). It is one of the more rounded options in its bracket — with its main drawback understood upfront: wired audio needs a usb-c adapter or dongle.

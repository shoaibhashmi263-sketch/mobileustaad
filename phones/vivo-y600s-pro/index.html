Overview

This Vivo is a China-market model, and its expected price of Rs 111,700 comes from a Chinese launch price of CNY 2,699 for the 8GB/256GB version; no Pakistan launch has been announced. The 6.83-inch AMOLED screen delivers a 1.5K resolution, 120Hz refresh rate, HDR and P3 wide colour. Despite a 10,200mAh typical battery capacity, the phone keeps IP68/IP69 ratings, stereo speakers, an IR blaster and NFC, though it weighs 221g.

Performance comes from the Dimensity 7300e, a 4nm chip clocked up to 2.5GHz with a Mali-G615 MC2 GPU, rated Good for gaming and able to handle popular titles at high settings. The camera story is deliberately simple: a single 50MP rear camera with EIS and a 32MP front camera. That means no ultrawide for group shots and no telephoto for zoom, so this is a point-and-shoot arrangement rather than a versatile system.

Charging is a highlight at 90W wired, with reverse wired charging also available, which suits a battery of this size far better than slow adapters. Software is Android 16 with the China version of OriginOS 6, and Vivo has not published an update policy for it. Choose it if extreme battery capacity and fast charging outweigh everything else; skip it if you want multiple rear cameras or software built for markets outside China.

Gaming review

The Dimensity 7300e is a solid mid-range gaming chip that handles popular titles at high settings; the 120Hz AMOLED keeps motion smooth, though the huge battery is the real star for long sessions.

Our Verdict

The Vivo Y600s Pro is a premium mid-range phone from Vivo, and its strongest suit is clear: smooth 120Hz AMOLED (2800x1260 1.5K, 120Hz, HDR, P3 wide color, 1.07 billion colors, in-display fingerprint) display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the MediaTek Dimensity 7300e (4nm, octa-core up to 2.5GHz, Mali-G615 MC2) chipset and a 10,200mAh battery with 90W charging. It runs Android 16 (OriginOS 6, China) out of the box. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — bearing in mind its clearest weakness: the 3.5mm headphone jack is missing.

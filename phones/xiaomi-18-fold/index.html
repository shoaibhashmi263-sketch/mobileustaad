Overview

The Xiaomi 18 Fold is Xiaomi's 2026 flagship book-style foldable, launched in China on September 7, 2026. It opens into a 7.58-inch 120Hz foldable AMOLED (4:3-like tablet ratio) with a 5.38-inch 120Hz cover screen, runs Xiaomi's in-house 3nm Xring O3 chip, and carries a Leica triple camera led by a 200MP main sensor. Despite a 6,000mAh battery, it is just 5.02mm thin unfolded and weighs 219g, with 67W wired and 50W wireless charging. China pricing starts at CNY 10,999; no Pakistan or global launch has been announced.

Gaming review

Xiaomi's 3nm Xring O3 trades blows with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme in AnTuTu, and the 7.58-inch 120Hz inner display turns the 18 Fold into a tablet-class gaming device. The 6,000mAh battery and 67W charging keep long sessions practical, though sustained-load thermals on a foldable remain the question until independent testing. Our Verdict

The Xiaomi 18 Fold is a phone from Xiaomi, and its strongest suit is clear: smooth 120Hz foldable AMOLED (inner 7.58-inch 120Hz with flexible UTG; cover 5.38-inch AMOLED 120Hz with Dragon Crystal Glass 3.0) display that feels fast in everyday use. It packs the Xiaomi Xring O3 (3nm; 10-core CPU; top 16GB/1TB config is the first phone with LPDDR6 RAM) chipset and a 6,000mAh battery with 67W charging. On the software side it runs Android 17. It earns a recommendation for most buyers in its class — just go in knowing its main trade-off: xiaomi has not promised a software update period for this model.

How good is the Xiaomi 18 Fold camera?

The rear setup is 200 MP Leica main (1/1.56-inch, f/1.7, OIS) + 50 MP periscope telephoto (3.5x optical) + 50 MP ultrawide, and the front camera is 16MP.

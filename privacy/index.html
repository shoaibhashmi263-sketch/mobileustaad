Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026. Mobile Ustaad ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact form: when you send us a message, we receive your name, email address, subject and message so we can reply.
  • Comments: when you leave a comment on a phone page, we store the name and comment text you provide. Comments are published only after moderation.
  • Analytics: we use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which pages are popular (pages viewed, approximate location by country, device type). This data is aggregated and never identifies you personally.

2. Cookies and advertising

We use cookies to keep the site working (for example, remembering your compare list). If we show third-party ads in the future (e.g. Google AdSense), those vendors may use cookies — including the DoubleClick cookie — to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your messages and fix reported price/spec errors.
  • To moderate and display comments you choose to post.
  • To improve the site's content and performance.

We never sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Third-party services

Our site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may process basic technical data (such as IP addresses) to deliver pages securely and prevent abuse. Embedded content (for example, YouTube trailers) is governed by the privacy policies of those providers.

5. Data retention

Contact messages are kept for up to 12 months. Comments you post remain visible until you ask us to remove them.

6. Children's privacy

Mobile Ustaad is a general-audience information site and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will always show the latest version.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us through our contact page.

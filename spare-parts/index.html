Mobile Spare Parts Prices in Pakistan

Typical market prices for the replacement parts Pakistani repair shops fit every day — batteries, displays, cameras, charging parts and small components — grouped by type. Each part page lists the models it fits, the signs that the part actually needs replacing, a price breakdown by quality grade, typical fitting labour, and where to find the part.

Two habits save money in every repair market from Hall Road Lahore to Saddar Karachi: confirm the exact part number for your model before buying (look-alike parts for other models rarely fit), and ask whether the quote includes fitting. Prices here are market estimates that vary by city and by quality grade — copy, aftermarket and original-grade parts are priced differently, and each part page explains its grades.

About this part

The iPhone 12 Display (OLED) is the replacement display assembly for iPhone 12 (OLED, 6.1-inch). For most models, Pakistan's markets offer several display quality grades, original-grade OLED, decent copy panels, and budget TFT/INCELL options, the grade decides brightness, colour, touch response and — on phones with an in-display fingerprint reader — whether the fingerprint sensor keeps working, so always ask which grade you are being quoted for.

Signs it needs replacing

  • Cracks or shattered glass, even if the touch still works
  • Touch not responding on part of the screen, or ghost touches
  • Black spots, coloured lines or a spreading ink-like patch
  • Screen flickering or brightness uneven across the panel
  • Display completely blank while the phone still rings or vibrates

Total repair cost estimate

The part itself typically costs Rs 15,000 – Rs 22,000 in Pakistan. Adding fitting labor, most customers pay around Rs 15,800 – Rs 23,500 in total for this repair at a local shop. Service-centre fitting costs more but uses genuine parts where available.

Price breakdown

Apple sells no retail displays. Re-checked 2026-11-24: one aftermarket OLED unit at Rs 13,250 (AdvanceStore.pk) sits below the range floor; single brand only — range kept pending a second source.

Buying and fitting tips

Do not pay in full until you have tested corner-to-corner touch, daylight brightness, and the fingerprint reader if it sits in the display. A frame-fitted assembly costs more, yet aligns better and resists later cracking. Hold on to your old display until you are satisfied; a panel failing in the first days should be replaced under the shop's fitting warranty.

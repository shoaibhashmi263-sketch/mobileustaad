About this part

The Redmi Note 11 Display is the replacement display assembly for Redmi Note 11 (AMOLED, 6.43-inch). Pakistan's markets sell several quality grades for the same model: original-grade OLED panels, good-quality copies, and cheaper TFT/INCELL panels. The grade decides brightness, colour, touch response and — on phones with an in-display fingerprint reader — whether the fingerprint sensor keeps working, so always ask which grade you are being quoted for.

Signs it needs replacing

  • Cracks or shattered glass, even if the touch still works
  • Touch not responding on part of the screen, or ghost touches
  • Black spots, coloured lines or a spreading ink-like patch
  • Screen flickering or brightness uneven across the panel
  • Display completely blank while the phone still rings or vibrates

Total repair cost estimate

The part itself typically costs Rs 8,500 – Rs 12,800 in Pakistan. Adding fitting labor, most customers pay around Rs 9,300 – Rs 14,000 in total for this repair at a local shop. Service-centre fitting costs more but uses genuine parts where available.

Buying and fitting tips

Before paying in full, test touch response on every corner, maximum brightness in daylight, and the fingerprint reader if your phone has one in the display. Frame-fitted assemblies cost more but align better and crack less easily later. Keep your old display until you are satisfied — if the new panel fails in the first days, the shop should replace it under its fitting warranty.

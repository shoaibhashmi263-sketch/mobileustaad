About this part

The Samsung Galaxy A12 Display is the replacement display assembly for Samsung Galaxy A12 (PLS LCD, 6.5-inch). The same model's display comes in several quality grades in local markets, original-grade OLED, decent copy panels, and budget TFT/INCELL options, your grade decides the brightness, colour accuracy, touch feel and, on phones with in-display fingerprint readers, whether the sensor still works, so never compare two quotes without asking the grade behind each.

Signs it needs replacing

  • Cracks or shattered glass, even if the touch still works
  • Touch not responding on part of the screen, or ghost touches
  • Black spots, coloured lines or a spreading ink-like patch
  • Screen flickering or brightness uneven across the panel
  • Display completely blank while the phone still rings or vibrates

Buying and fitting tips

Do not pay in full until you have tested corner-to-corner touch, daylight brightness, and the fingerprint reader if it sits in the display. Frame-fitted assemblies are pricier but sit better and crack less easily afterwards. Keep your old display until you are satisfied — if the new panel fails in the first days, the shop should replace it under its fitting warranty.

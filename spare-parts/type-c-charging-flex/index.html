About this part

The Type-C Charging Port Flex (Generic) is the charging-port part for Most Type-C Android phones (model-specific flex varies). On many phones the charging port sits on a small sub-board (flex) that also carries the microphone and sometimes the headphone jack, so one faulty port can cause several symptoms at once. Cheaper flex boards may charge slowly or drop fast-charging support, which is why the part grade matters here more than the fitting cost.

Signs it needs replacing

  • Charging cable only works when held at a certain angle
  • Charging port feels loose and the plug wobbles
  • Charging has become very slow even with the original charger and cable
  • Computer no longer detects the phone over USB
  • People say your voice fades during calls (the microphone often shares this board)

Total repair cost estimate

The part itself typically costs Rs 200 – Rs 1,000 in Pakistan. Adding fitting labor, most customers pay around Rs 500 – Rs 1,800 in total for this repair at a local shop. Service-centre fitting costs more but uses genuine parts where available.

Buying and fitting tips

First rule out the simple causes: try a known-good cable and charger, and have the port cleaned — lint packed inside the port is a very common cause of loose, slow charging and is far cheaper to fix. If the board does need replacing, confirm after fitting that fast charging still engages and the microphone works on a test call.
